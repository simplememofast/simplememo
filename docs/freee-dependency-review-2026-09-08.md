# freee dependency review

Recorded September 8, 2026; public sources read September 7. Existing company and integration records establish freee as an accounting dependency. The inventory now lists it as pending review, without contract or payment approval. This is AI preparation for review, not a legal opinion or evidence of account acceptance.

Read all 30 articles, article 23-2 and supplementary provisions of the [general terms](https://www.freee.co.jp/terms/), last updated January 1, 2025. Individual terms take precedence. Application and acceptance form the contract; fees are specified separately and early termination generally does not produce refunds. Synchronised information is not guaranteed accurate or complete. Customers retain backup responsibilities; termination can lead to deletion. Service interruptions, changes and termination are contemplated. The liability clause limits recovery and uses one month's service fees as its cap; the consumer exception must not silently be applied to a company. Service IP remains with freee or its licensors. Information-use and outsourcing provisions need reconciliation with the actual accounting data and service configuration. Japanese law and Tokyo District Court are specified. These findings do not establish the account's applicable version, individual terms, subscription, payment authority or negotiated exceptions.

Read the substantive [privacy policy](https://www.freee.co.jp/privacy_policy/), revised July 1, 2026, including outbound-provider tables. It covers account, financial, uploaded and usage information; purposes include service operation, improvement and marketing. Outsourcing, group use and overseas processing require scope review. Some use can continue after the contract ends. Listed destinations do not prove that every provider receives this company's actual records. No network or tenant-setting audit was performed.

Read the complete [safety-management statement](https://www.freee.co.jp/privacy_policy/safety-management/). It describes responsibility, training, access controls, logging, encryption, incident procedures and consideration of foreign processing environments. These are published commitments, not independently verified operating controls or a tested recovery guarantee.

Remaining evidence: account-specific acceptance and amendments; individual/API terms and plan entitlement; actual data routing and deletion/export behaviour; DPA/SLA applicability; invoices, payment reconciliation and shared-cost allocation. CLM, CRM, email, chat and the account's stored contract originals were not searched in this review. A prior authenticated balance-job observation does not close those gaps. Contract originals remain in the owner's designated storage; no invoices or private account details are added here. Legal conclusions should be checked against originals by a qualified professional.
