Key points

The Captio method (Captio-style) is a note-taking approach designed for continuity: capture a thought briefly in 1 line, then write up and organize your notes later. It originated in the one-tap note-to-email workflow of the iOS app Captio, which shut down on 2024-10-1. This page explains the method. If you are looking for a Captio alternative app, see /captio-alternative/.

For those who are looking for a replacement for the Captio app (iOS): Captio is2024 year 10 service ends in month. The comparison and migration procedure for alternative apps is Captio Alternative App + Migration Guide . This page explains the Captio note-taking method.

Definition of Captio formula (in 3 line)

Captio formulais a memo technique designed on the premise that the moment you think of it is "throw it short (1 line memo)" and then "clear and organize" it together.
I don't try to write perfectly on the spot, so even while moving, spilling is reduced and the organization continues.
By adding "next action" to the end of the clear book, the thought log will change to action as it is.

Key points to understand in 3 minutes

1 line memo is not "information" but "hook"
The role of the 1 line memo is not a perfect record. It is a "reminiscence hook" when writing later. So it's good to be short.
The clear book is "put together roughly" rather than "perfect every time"
1 You don't have to clean each letter. 1 It is enough to look back together at the end of the day or on the weekend and organize it roughly.
"Next action" at the end of the book
It's a waste to organize and end up. " Soe, what are you going to do next? Just write the 1 line, and the memo will change to a task.
If you get lost, use the template
"I don't know what to write" is the biggest enemy. If you use a template, you can throw it without hesitation.See the template collection →

The reason why the Captio formula works

Lower the cognitive load
Don't think too much on the spot. " By "throwing for the time being", you can free up working memory and concentrate on the work in front of you.
Release the friction of recording
Start, write, and send it soon. No folder selection or tagging is required. So you can take notes while walking on the train.
Materials that can be judged later remain
1 line notes accumulate in the inbox = thoughts logs accumulate naturally. You can see "what you were thinking at that time" at the time of Kiyosho.
Leads to action
With the habit of adding "next action" at the end of the clear book, the memo will be "end of doing" instead of "reading and ending".

Common mistakes (and countermeasures)

  • Stop trying to write a long sentence on the spot
    If you say "Let's write properly" while moving, you get off the train on the way or get distracted by something else and end up not taking notes. The core of the Captio formula is "not to write perfectly".
    Countermeasures: 1 line, maximum 3 line. Ignore the appearance. Throw it on the premise of clearing the book later.
  • I can't get time to clean the book and accumulate
    If you try to carefully clear the 1 letters one by one, you will be frustrated with unprocessed notes. Perfectionism is the biggest enemy of the Captio formula.
    Countermeasures: It's OK to "collect and roughly". 1 times a week, only 15 minutes. Just throw away unnecessary things and add the 1 line action to the rest.
  • Clean the book and be satisfied and do not task
    The moment you put it together cleanly, you become "do it" and do not put it into actual action. Kiyosho is not the goal, but the starting point.
    Countermeasures: Be sure to write "Next thing to do: ○○" on the 1 line at the end of the clear book. If you don't have it, the clear book is incomplete.
  • Distribute because you don't know where to save it
    Memo app, Notion, Slack draft, Apple Notes... If there are multiple storage destinations, "Where did you write that?" A problem occurred.
    Countermeasures: The destination is unified to 1 (email inbox). With SimpleMemo, you won't get lost by fixing the addressee.
  • Tagging and organizing are too detailed and will go bankrupt
    Color coding, tags, folders, project linking... Even if it's fun at first, it becomes troublesome after 1 weeks and I stop it all.
    Countermeasures: Only the 2 classification of "cleared or unprocessed" is organized. If it's Gmail, just archive it.
