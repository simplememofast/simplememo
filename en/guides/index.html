Get the Most Out of Simple Memo - for Obsidian

Simple Memo - for Obsidian is built on a simple concept: email memos to yourself. But within that simplicity lies deep compatibility with productivity methods like GTD (Getting Things Done) capture phase, PKM (Personal Knowledge Management) input stage, and Inbox Zero practices.

This page organizes all Simple Memo - for Obsidian guides into three categories. If you need to get started, check "Setup Guides." To improve your daily workflow, see "Workflow Guides." If you're switching from another memo app, browse "Migration Guides."

Each guide takes less than 5 minutes to read. Start with whichever one catches your interest.
