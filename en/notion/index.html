Notion integration

Write or speak a thought and, in addition to sending the email, it is saved as one page in a “SimpleMemo Inbox” in Notion. You never have to open Notion to add to it, and typing, voice, Siri and Apple Watch all take the same path.

How it flows

Write / speak (typing, voice, Siri, Apple Watch)
      ↓
  Held on device   ← done here. You can write the next memo already
      ↓
  Email sent   +   one page added to the “SimpleMemo Inbox” in Notion
                   (and appended to your vault, if Obsidian is on)

You can write the next memo as soon as it is held on device. “Saved to Notion” appears only once Notion returns a page ID. Anything not saved yet stays on the device and can be retried from History.

What it does not do

This section exists so that nothing here is overstated. None of the following is implemented.

Not availableState
Bringing changes from Notion back into the app (two-way sync)Not available. The role is one-way saving to Notion only
Choosing which database to save intoNot available. Memos go to the “SimpleMemo Inbox” created on connect
Tagging, automatic classification, rewriting the textNot available. What you wrote is what is saved
AttachmentsNot available
Starting without emailNot possible. An email destination is a prerequisite. Only the Premium “Save to Notion only” setting removes it
Choosing Notion during first-run setupNot possible. First run only sets the email destination. Notion is connected from Settings

What we deliberately do not own

There is no Notion viewer and no Notion editor inside the app. Notion is a destination; this app only writes to it. Not adding more screens to organise things in is a deliberate choice.

Privacy

  • Your Notion access token is never returned to the device. It is encrypted and held server-side.
  • Delivery records keep metadata only — there is no column that holds the memo text.
  • Memos not yet sent stay in the existing encrypted outbox on the device.
  • Account deletion removes the server-side data for each stored and disconnected Notion connection first. Pages inside Notion are not deleted.

Getting started

  1. Open Settings in the app
  2. Turn on Notion → “Notion integration”
  3. Approve on Notion’s authorisation screen (one “SimpleMemo Inbox” is created in your private space)

From then on, memos go to both email and Notion. To stop sending email and use Notion alone, use “Save to Notion only” on the same screen (Premium).

FAQ

Can I use it together with the Obsidian integration?

Yes. With both on, a memo goes to email, Notion and Obsidian. Turning on “Save to Notion only” stops the Obsidian append.

Do other apps already do this?

Other apps can save to Notion. Nothing here claims to be a world first or the only one. What this app offers is that typing, voice, Siri and Apple Watch all take the same path.

What if I cannot tell whether it saved?

“Saved to Notion” appears only once a Notion page ID is confirmed. Anything unconfirmed stays in History, where you can copy the text or send it again.
