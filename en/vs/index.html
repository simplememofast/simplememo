TL;DR

Simple Memo is an iOS memo-to-email app built as a Captio alternative (Captio's cloud service ended in October 2024). It launches in 0.4 seconds (warm launch, iPhone 16e), sends with one tap, uses AES-GCM on-device encryption, does not persistently store memo content on servers, and includes offline Outbox auto-resend. Developed by YURIKA, K.K. (Japan) as an independent app — not Captio's official successor.

What is Simple Memo - for Obsidian?

Simple Memo - for Obsidian is an iOS app built around one goal: capture a thought and send it with one tap (it is ready to type about 0.4 seconds after the tap in the developer's warm-launch measurement on iPhone 16e). It has no folders or manual filing — memos arrive in your email inbox and, if you set up the integration, are appended to a note in your Obsidian vault; with AI Auto-Tagging turned on, on-device AI adds a title and tags to that note. It keeps memos sent while offline in an Outbox, encrypts on-device data with AES-GCM, and sends with one tap. It was designed as a Captio alternative, inspired by the workflow of the discontinued Captio app.

Which Memo App Should You Choose?

Speed First → Simple Memo
For people who want to note ideas the moment they come. Ready to type 0.4s after the tap (developer's measurement, warm launch), and one tap sends it to your email.
Organization First → Apple Notes / Notion
Need folders, tags, search, and rich text editing? Apple Notes or Notion are better choices, though launch speed and simplicity suffer.
Privacy First → Standard Notes / Simple Memo
For content that must stay unreadable to others, Standard Notes' end-to-end encryption fits better. Simple Memo needs no Google account, does not persistently store memo bodies on our servers, and encrypts with AES-GCM on the device (sent memos are ordinary emails in your inbox, so this is not end-to-end encryption).
Team Sharing → Notion / Slack
For team collaboration, real-time editing, and project management, Notion or Slack are purpose-built. Simple Memo is for personal instant capture only.

FAQ

What is the most important criterion when choosing a memo app?

It depends on your use case. For quick capture, launch speed and one-tap sending matter most. For information organization, prioritize folders, tags, and search. For team collaboration, focus on sharing and real-time editing features.

How is Simple Memo different from other memo apps?

Simple Memo is purpose-built for one thing: be ready to type 0.4 seconds after the tap (warm launch, iPhone 16e), then send the thought with one tap. It has no folders or manual filing — memos arrive in your email inbox and, if you set up the integration, are appended to a note in your Obsidian vault (with AI Auto-Tagging on, on-device AI adds a title and tags to that note).

Can I use multiple memo apps together?

Yes. For example, you can use Simple Memo for instant capture and Apple Notes or Notion for long-form writing and organization. Using tools for their strengths is a great productivity strategy.

How is Simple Memo different from LINE Keep Memo?

First, the facts: Keep Memo itself still works — what ended in August 2024 was LINE Keep, the storage feature. Simple Memo also captures quickly, but your memos are kept in your own email inbox (and in your Obsidian vault if you set up the integration) rather than in LINE. How long they stay in your inbox depends on your email service and settings.

Which memo app has the best offline support?

Simple Memo uses Outbox architecture — memos sent while offline are encrypted with AES-GCM and auto-sent when connectivity returns. Apple Notes also has offline support via iCloud. Google Keep and Notion have limited offline capabilities.
