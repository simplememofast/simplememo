TL;DR

Simple Memo is an iOS memo-to-email app built as a successor to Captio (which shut down in October 2024). It launches in 0.4 seconds, sends with one tap, uses AES-GCM on-device encryption, stores zero memo content on servers, and includes offline Outbox auto-resend. Developed by YURIKA, K.K. (Japan) as an independent app — not Captio's official successor.

What is Simple Memo - for Obsidian?

Simple Memo - for Obsidian is an iOS app built entirely around one goal: capture a thought and send it to email in under one second. It has no folders, tags, or management features — your email inbox is the storage layer. Built with Outbox architecture for offline reliability, AES-GCM encryption, and one-tap send, it was designed as a replacement in the spirit of the discontinued Captio app.

Which Memo App Should You Choose?

Speed First → Simple Memo
Fastest launch at 0.4s, one-tap email send. Built for people who think fast and want to capture every idea instantly.
Organization First → Apple Notes / Notion
Need folders, tags, search, and rich text editing? Apple Notes or Notion are better choices, though launch speed and simplicity suffer.
Privacy First → Simple Memo
No Google account, memos never stored on server, AES-GCM encryption. Privacy-by-design architecture beats cloud-sync apps.
Team Sharing → Notion / Slack
For team collaboration, real-time editing, and project management, Notion or Slack are purpose-built. Simple Memo is for personal instant capture only.

FAQ

What is the most important criterion when choosing a memo app?

It depends on your use case. For quick capture, launch speed and one-tap sending matter most. For information organization, prioritize folders, tags, and search. For team collaboration, focus on sharing and real-time editing features.

How is Simple Memo different from other memo apps?

Simple Memo is purpose-built for one thing: capture a thought in 0.4 seconds and send it to email with one tap. It has no folders, tags, or management features. Your email inbox is the only storage layer.

Can I use multiple memo apps together?

Yes. Many users use Simple Memo for instant capture and Apple Notes or Notion for long-form writing and organization. Using tools for their strengths is a great productivity strategy.

How is Simple Memo different from LINE Keep Memo?

First, the facts: Keep Memo itself still works — what ended in August 2024 was LINE Keep, the storage feature. Simple Memo provides similar quick-capture functionality with the added benefit of email delivery, creating a permanent record outside of any single service's ecosystem.

Which memo app has the best offline support?

Simple Memo uses Outbox architecture — memos written offline are encrypted with AES-GCM and auto-sent when connectivity returns. Apple Notes also has offline support via iCloud. Google Keep and Notion have limited offline capabilities.
