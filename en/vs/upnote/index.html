Simple Memo vs UpNote
Which Should You Choose?

Consider UpNote for editing and organizing notes inside one app across devices. Consider Simple Memo when you want to capture a short note for your own inbox or configured Obsidian destination. Compare the entry point, destination and export path using the same note.

By AI ATAKA · Published: March 6, 2026

Updated September 9, 2026. The author develops Simple Memo. This comparison uses the official sources below; it does not report a controlled speed test or a user preference survey.

Simple Memo pricing and free allowance

UpNote also offers a direct capture route

On mobile, use the blue new-note button below the list. The first line becomes the title, so a separate title field is not required. On desktop, use Cmd/Ctrl + N. From another app, use Cmd + Option + N on Mac or Ctrl + Alt + N on Windows/Linux to open a new note window.

Do not calculate a speed advantage by assuming notebook selection is mandatory every time. Create one note through your usual entry point and find it again. If your workflow ends in an inbox, include reading the delivered Simple Memo message in the comparison.

UpNote: creating a note and its title

Check the limit, purchase type and cancellation

The free plan allows up to 50 notes. Check Premium requirements for attachments, locking, importing, and PDF or Markdown export. Use the price shown in your regional store for either monthly or Lifetime access. Purchase on Mac, iOS or Android, then sign in with the same UpNote account on Windows/Linux; direct upgrades are not offered there.

After a monthly subscription expires, existing notes remain accessible, but you cannot create new ones above the 50-note limit and some export features are unavailable. If you plan to migrate, test the required export format while the subscription is active.

UpNote: Premium and cancellation conditions

Test Evernote links and attachments before migration

The desktop app imports Evernote ENEX files. Export each original notebook separately and enable “Use file name as notebook” to create the corresponding groups; arrange nesting after import. When converting tags to hashtags, special characters may become hyphens.

Evernote internal links still open Evernote rather than becoming UpNote links. Attachments above 20 MB or notes above 300,000 characters may fail to import. Inspect colors and layout, and retain the original ENEX. Bulk imports that would exceed 20,000 notes or 10 GB of attachments are limited to 50 notes per batch.

UpNote: ENEX import and limitations

Separate sync from a recoverable copy

Automatic backups are local to Mac, Windows and Linux. Defaults are once a day with up to 50 days retained; check frequency, location and attachment settings. If you mainly use mobile, confirm that notes have synced to a desktop and a backup exists. Do not edit the managed files inside its backup folder.

UpNote: automatic backup

For another app, export a small sample as Markdown or HTML and open the text, images and links. Having a backup does not prove another app can reproduce every feature. Messages delivered by Simple Memo also depend on your email provider’s storage, retention and backup settings.

UpNote: export formats and controls

Without signing in, UpNote keeps notes on the device; syncing adds storage on Firebase servers in the US. Encryption in transit and at rest, or an app lock, should not be treated as end-to-end encryption that prevents the service from decrypting content.

UpNote: storage and privacy

Five checks from the free plan to migration

  1. Choose one representative short note and enter it using your usual device and entry point.
  2. Find it as if returning the next day, add a line, and check its destination.
  3. Open the needed note, read and edit offline, then check another device after reconnection.
  4. If importing is necessary, first check the Premium features and purchase terms. Once you have access, import a small sample with images, attachments and internal links, then compare with the original.
  5. Export in a format available under your plan, then check the files and backup location before a full migration.

Start with UpNote alone if you want to keep editing inside the notes app. Consider Simple Memo if you process work in email. Moving received mail into UpNote requires a separate copy or supported import step; using both apps does not establish an automatic integration.

Frequently asked questions

What does UpNote cost now?
The official site lists US$1.99/month or US$39.99 Lifetime. Region and tax vary, so check the final in-app price in your store.
What happens at the free 50-note limit?
Premium is required to add notes beyond the limit. After a monthly plan expires, existing notes remain accessible, but new notes above 50 and some Premium features are unavailable.
Must I select a notebook before every new note?
No. Use the mobile new-note button or a desktop shortcut. The first line becomes the title, so there is no required separate title-entry step.
Does UpNote work offline?
You can read and write offline. With signed-in sync, changes reach other devices after reconnection. Open required notes and attachments on your actual device before travel.
Is note locking end-to-end encryption?
No. UpNote documents encryption in transit and at rest, but explicitly states that it does not support E2EE. App or note locking does not change that distinction.
Do Evernote internal links migrate too?
They are not converted into UpNote links and continue to open Evernote. Test a sample because attachment size, long notes, tag conversion and formatting also have limitations.
For an inbox-based capture workflow
Write and send one note, then check the delivered text. The free allowance is three sends per day.
