Capture vs Organize — Choose the right tool

Simple Memo vs Bear
Full Comparison

Bear is a beautiful Markdown editor for writing and organizing long-form notes. Simple Memo is an instant-capture tool that sends your thought to email in one tap. Both are "note apps," but they serve entirely different purposes. Find out which one matches your workflow.

By AI ATAKA · Published: March 5, 2026

As a developer who thoroughly tested both apps' UIs, I found Bear's beautiful Markdown editor ideal for writing, but Simple Memo's one-tap send was overwhelmingly more comfortable for capturing single-line thoughts.

The Core Difference: Capture vs Organize

Bear is designed for writing and organizing notes beautifully. It offers Markdown support, tags, folders, iCloud sync, and elegant themes — everything a writer or knowledge worker needs for long-form content. Simple Memo is designed for instant capture and email delivery. It launches in 0.4 seconds, sends with one tap, and clears after sending — a three-step flow optimized solely for not losing a thought. These apps are complementary, not competing: use Simple Memo for capture, Bear for organization.

3 Craft-Level Differences

DIFFERENCE 01
Writing Experience: Instant Capture vs Beautiful Composition
Bear cherishes the "writing experience" itself — beautiful themes, fonts, and Markdown formatting. Simple Memo prioritizes "speed that prevents losing a thought" over writing experience. ~1-second launch, one-tap send. A design that chose reliability over beauty.
DIFFERENCE 02
Markdown vs Plain Text: Formatting Philosophy
Bear's full Markdown support is ideal for structured writing with headings, lists, and code blocks. But for a fleeting idea, Markdown syntax is unnecessary overhead. Simple Memo is plain text only — zero cognitive load from formatting, letting you capture raw thoughts as they come.
DIFFERENCE 03
Apple-Only vs Email Universality
Bear is Apple-ecosystem only (iOS, iPadOS, macOS) with iCloud sync dependency. Simple Memo is also iOS-only, but since output goes to email, received memos are accessible from Windows, Android, Linux — any device with email. No platform restriction on the "reading side" of your notes.

Bear 2's Subscription Shift and Alternatives

Bear 2 launched in 2023 with a major shift from one-time purchase to subscription pricing ($2.99/month or $29.99/year). Some existing users feel annual payment for a note app is steep, prompting a search for alternatives.

Simple Memo is not a complete Bear replacement. It's not a Markdown editor and has no tag management. However, for the single purpose of "quickly jotting and emailing a note," it's dramatically faster than Bear, and the free plan (3 sends/day) is surprisingly practical. If you're hesitating on Bear's subscription renewal, consider a "hybrid strategy": move daily quick memos to Simple Memo while keeping Bear for long-form writing and Markdown needs. You don't have to give up Bear's beautiful editor.

Who Should Choose Which

Choose Bear If...
  • You write notes in Markdown
  • You organize notes with tags and folders
  • You need seamless Mac/iPhone/iPad sync
  • You write long-form articles, diaries, or docs
  • You appreciate beautiful UI and rich themes
Choose Simple Memo If...
  • You need to capture thoughts in under a second
  • You want notes stored permanently in email
  • You want notes accessible without a specific app
  • You need reliable offline note capture
  • You want zero-friction, single-screen simplicity

FAQ

Is Bear's Markdown or Simple Memo's plain text easier to use?

It depends on the task. For structured articles and documents, Bear's Markdown is far superior. For capturing a quick thought during a meeting, Markdown syntax is unnecessary overhead. Simple Memo's plain text lets you record thoughts without any formatting cognitive load.

Can I email a Bear note directly?

Bear has no direct email send — you must use the share menu manually. Simple Memo sends via email in one tap. If emailing notes is part of your daily routine, Simple Memo requires fewer steps.

I love Bear's themes and design. What does Simple Memo look like?

Bear is renowned for its beautiful themes and typography. Simple Memo has a minimal UI — just an input field and send button, with no decorative elements. Bear's "joy of writing" and Simple Memo's "speed of writing" are different values, and using both is the ideal approach.

Does Bear work on non-Apple devices?

Bear is Apple-only (iOS, iPadOS, macOS) — no Windows or Android. Simple Memo is also iOS-only, but sent memos arrive as email, readable from Windows, Android, Linux, or any device with an email client.

Which is more usable without Bear Pro subscription?

Bear's free version limits device sync and export formats. Simple Memo's free tier has a 3-send-per-day limit with no feature restrictions. For trying out, Simple Memo's free experience is more complete.
