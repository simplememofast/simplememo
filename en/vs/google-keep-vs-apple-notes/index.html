Google Keep vs Apple Notes
Which Should You Choose?

Choose Google Keep if Android is part of your everyday setup and you want shared notes and lists. Choose Apple Notes if folders, documents and your Apple devices are central. You can also access iCloud notes in a web browser. Check reminder, sharing and encryption settings before deciding.

By AI Ataka · Published: March 6, 2026

Reviewed September 9, 2026. The author develops Simple Memo. This comparison uses official documentation; it is not a new same-device speed benchmark.

Both can be used without an app subscription; account, storage and organization policies still apply. Do not choose from an app-only price or a single launch-time claim.

Can I use Apple Notes on Windows or share with Android?

Apple documents web access to iCloud notes. That is useful when you need a note from a computer, but browser access should not be treated as identical to every feature of the native apps. With Advanced Data Protection, web data access is off by default and requires a separate choice.

For an Android-and-iPhone household, try a shared Keep list with each person’s account. In Apple Notes, collaboration requires the supported Apple account and device setup; use the note’s menu to remove its lock before collaboration; temporarily opening it with Face ID is not the same as removing the lock. Sending a copy is different from letting someone edit the same live note.

Labels or folders, and the text you need to write

Keep’s labels, colors and pins help group small notes. Labels and colors are personal even on a shared note. Keep also documents headings, bold, italic and underline on the web, so “Keep has no text formatting” is no longer an accurate shortcut for the comparison.

For meeting notes and documents, try a folder and a longer note in Apple Notes. For shopping or quick reference, try a list and a label in Keep. Compare finding the note a day later, not only writing it once.

Keep reminders moved to Google Tasks

Google’s migration guide says new Keep reminders are saved as tasks. Mobile alerts come from Tasks or Calendar, with notification permission enabled. Keep no longer offers location-based reminders.

If a reminder seems missing, open Tasks or Calendar, check the task and its date, then test a short future reminder. This change concerns reminders; it is not evidence that Keep notes have shut down.

Compare encryption settings, not a “most secure” label

Google describes Keep encryption in transit and at rest. It stores content on its infrastructure and processes content to provide features such as recognition and search. This is different from a promise that only your devices can decrypt every note.

Apple’s iCloud security table lists ordinary Notes as encrypted in transit and on the server under standard protection. Advanced Data Protection changes Notes to end-to-end encryption when enabled. Shared content has participant requirements, and some metadata remains under standard protection. Check availability, recovery settings and the exact sharing setup you intend to use.

Local storage, a note lock, cloud encryption and email encryption address different parts of a workflow. Simple Memo encrypts its on-device Outbox and history; sent mail still passes through delivery infrastructure. See its privacy explanation.

Test export and migration with a small sample

  1. Keep a source copy. Keep’s export guide includes note content, attachments, labels and other note data. Work or school accounts may restrict downloads.
  2. Check the destination format. Apple supports Markdown import on iOS 26, iPadOS 26 and macOS Tahoe 26 or later. Its current iPhone export guide also describes Markdown output. An archive from another app may need conversion before import.
  3. Try a few representative notes. Include a checklist, an image and a longer note. Compare text, attachments, dates and organization after the move. Keep the source until the result is usable.

For travel, open the notes and attachments you need while online, then check they reopen with your connection off. Sync and export are not substitutes for a separate backup. See the offline preparation guide.

If your destination is your own inbox

You can share or copy text from your existing notes into your usual email app. If reviewing an inbox is already your routine, Simple Memo offers a separate iPhone capture workflow. Configure and verify the recipient, then confirm a test message arrives. Free use permits 3 sends per day.

When offline, press Send and confirm the item is pending in the Outbox. After reconnecting, check the app and the receiving inbox. If you want the text in Keep or Apple Notes, move the selected text there during review; this is a manual workflow. Optional Obsidian saving requires its own integration and target setup.

Frequently asked questions

Is Google Keep or Apple Notes better for iPhone?

Start with the app you will also use to review your notes. Apple Notes suits folders and documents on Apple devices; Keep is a practical option when Android is part of your setup. Test one note and one shared list on every device you need before moving your archive.

Are all Apple Notes end-to-end encrypted by default?

No. For ordinary iCloud Notes, standard protection uses encryption in transit and on the server. Advanced Data Protection extends end-to-end encryption to Notes when enabled. Locked notes and shared notes have additional conditions; check the settings and the participants, not just the app name.

Why did Google Keep stop sending reminder notifications?

Google moved Keep reminders to Tasks. Use the Tasks or Calendar mobile app and allow notifications there. Location-based Keep reminders are no longer available. Check a short test reminder after configuring the app.

Can I move notes between Google Keep and Apple Notes?

Keep offers a downloadable archive through Google Takeout. Apple Notes supports Markdown import on iOS 26, iPadOS 26 and macOS Tahoe 26 or later; current iPhone Notes also offers Markdown export. An export is not a guaranteed one-step migration: check text, attachments and organization using a small sample first.

Do I need a third app to email a note to myself?

No. You can use the sharing or copy options already available in your note and email apps. Simple Memo is an optional iPhone workflow for a configured recipient; verify the address and test delivery first. Its free tier allows 3 sends per day. Moving received text into Keep or Notes is a separate step.
