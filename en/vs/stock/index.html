Simple Memo vs Stock
Which Should You Choose?

The fundamental difference between Simple Memo and Stock is individual vs team use. Stock is a team information sharing tool that centralizes notes, tasks, and messages. Simple Memo is a personal instant capture tool that opens in 0.4 seconds and sends to your email.

By AI ATAKA · Published: March 6, 2026

What is Stock?

Stock is a Japanese team information sharing tool. Its concept is "the easiest way to save team information," integrating notes, tasks, and messaging. Free for up to 5 users; business plans start at 500 yen/person/month. Known for its simple UI accessible to teams with varying IT literacy, used by over 200,000 Japanese companies.

3 Differences from Stock

  • 1Memo Storage (In-App vs Email)
    Stock saves memos in its team collaboration space. Simple Memo sends memos to your email inbox, giving you email's searchability, permanence, and cross-device access. For personal memos that don't need team sharing, email storage is simpler.
  • 2Team Use vs Individual Use
    Stock is a team information-sharing tool that centralizes notes, tasks, and messages. Simple Memo is purpose-built for individual "think it, capture it" moments. Need team sharing? Stock. Need personal idea capture? Simple Memo. The use cases are clearly distinct.
  • 3Search & Tags vs Inbox
    Stock organizes notes with folder structures and tags. Simple Memo sends memos to your email inbox, where your email client's powerful search can instantly find any past memo. It's dedicated app organization vs the search power of your existing email.

Stock for Teams vs Simple Memo - for Obsidian for Individuals

Stock and Simple Memo - for Obsidian solve fundamentally different problems. Stock is a team information-sharing and communication tool. It is designed for accumulating and sharing team information: meeting notes, manuals, task management, and member messaging. Simple Memo - for Obsidian, on the other hand, is purely personal. It is dedicated to one thing: capturing what you just thought of in 0.4 seconds and sending it to YOUR email inbox. It has zero team sharing features. If you need team features, use Stock. If you need instant personal capture that lands in your inbox, use Simple Memo - for Obsidian. In practice, the most effective approach is using Stock for team collaboration while adding Simple Memo - for Obsidian for personal idea capture.

When to Use Stock

Simple Memo is ideal for personal capture, but Stock is genuinely better in these scenarios. When team information sharing is required, Stock's strengths are overwhelming.

  • 1Team Meeting Notes and Manual Archival
    When you need meeting notes, operational manuals, and company rules accessible to the entire team, Stock's "Notes" feature excels. Organized with folder structures and easily searchable. Simple Memo only sends to personal email, so it is not suited for team sharing.
  • 2Information Sharing for Teams with Varying IT Skills
    Stock is designed with "usable by anyone" as its core concept. Even members who find Slack or Notion difficult can use Stock intuitively. It is especially suitable for Japanese small-to-medium businesses with team members of all ages and tech comfort levels.
  • 3Integrated Task Management and Messaging
    Stock unifies notes, tasks, and messaging in one platform. Task assignment, progress tracking, and team communication are centralized, eliminating the need to juggle multiple tools.

Migration Guide: Adding Simple Memo to Your Stock Workflow

The most effective approach is adding Simple Memo for personal idea capture while continuing to use Stock for team collaboration. Rather than a full migration, think of Simple Memo as adding a personal memo layer.

  • 1Download Simple Memo from the App Store
    The free version allows up to 3 sends per day. No Stock account needed — just an email address to get started. From download to first use takes under one minute.
  • 2Set Up Your Personal Email Address
    Configure a personal email address separate from Stock. By separating work and personal flows, you can safely accumulate unorganized ideas before team sharing.
  • 3Capture Ideas On-the-Go with Simple Memo
    Instantly capture ideas during commutes, lunch breaks, or before meetings. No need to open Stock and navigate to a team space. The cursor is live 0.4 seconds after the tap; typing and sending follow from there.
  • 4Organize From Email to Stock
    Review inbox memos and move team-relevant content into Stock notes. Keep personal items in email or archive them. This sorting process improves the quality of information posted to Stock.
  • 5Clearly Separate Team Info and Personal Notes
    Stock holds official team information; Simple Memo holds your personal raw ideas. This clear separation keeps Stock notes organized and easier for team members to navigate.

FAQ

Can I search Stock memos in my email inbox?

No, Stock memos are stored in-app and cannot be searched via email. Simple Memo sends memos to your email, so you can use Gmail or other email clients' powerful search to find past memos instantly.

Is Stock suitable for personal memos?

Stock is designed for teams, so it is overpowered for personal use. For individual idea capture, Simple Memo is simpler and launches in 0.4 seconds for immediate note-taking.

How do Stock and Simple Memo differ in UI complexity?

Stock offers a moderate UI with notes, tasks, messages, folders, and tags. Simple Memo has a minimal UI with just an input field and a send button, letting you focus entirely on capturing thoughts.

How can I use Stock and Simple Memo together?

Capture ideas instantly with Simple Memo during commutes or before meetings, then organize team-relevant content into Stock. This clearly separates personal memos from team information.
