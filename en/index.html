Simple Memo - for Obsidian is an email-yourself app for iPhone built for speed. In our benchmark of eight note apps it was the quickest to accept typing: 0.4 seconds after the tap (warm launch, iPhone 16e — how we measured). Then one tap sends the note to your inbox. Built as a Captio alternative after Captio shut down in October 2024. Also works as a LINE Keep replacement for text memos. Features fully on-device voice auto-input, Obsidian integration (auto-appends sent memos to your notes), on-device AES-GCM encryption for the Outbox and send history, offline auto-resend via Outbox architecture, and no persistent server storage of your memo text (transit only during send). Version 3.9 (July 2026) adds Apple Watch support — speak into your wrist and the memo lands in both your inbox and your Obsidian vault. A free update in July 2026 added AI auto-tagging: on-device AI (Apple Foundation Models) reads the note and adds a title of up to 20 characters, one to three tags, and a type (todo / idea / log), then appends it to Obsidian with those tags intact. The tagging runs entirely on your iPhone and memos are never sent to an external server for it (devices without Apple Intelligence fall back to on-device lightweight formatting). Far simpler than Drafts, with a faster measured launch.

Our Story Why We Built This

We were long-time Captio users. The ability to send a thought to your inbox with one tap — that simple experience was unlike any other memo app. But in October 2024, Captio shut down. We searched for an alternative with the same speed and simplicity, but nothing came close.

"If it doesn't exist, we'll build it ourselves." That decision launched this project. We reached a 0.4s launch (warm, iPhone 16e) and one-tap sends, built an Outbox that keeps notes written offline on the device and retries them, and designed a privacy-first system that does not persistently store your memos on our servers. Inspired by Captio's workflow and rebuilt with modern technology, we set out to make "note-to-email" as quick as we can.

Support FAQ

What is Simple Memo?

Simple Memo is a memo app focused on one thing: launch, write, send. Your memo is sent as an email to a pre-configured address with a single tap. There are no folders, sync settings, or complex features — and you never tag anything by hand, because AI auto-tagging does it on device.

What is AI auto-tagging?

Speak or type a note, and AI reads it and adds a title of up to 20 characters, one to three tags describing the content, and a type (todo / idea / log). It shipped as a free update in July 2026. The generated title is also used as the email subject, and with Obsidian integration on, the note is appended to your vault with those tags intact. Your original text is never rewritten. See AI auto-tagging for details.

Does AI tagging send my memos to an external server?

No. Tagging and title generation run on your iPhone using Apple's on-device AI (Apple Foundation Models), with no network calls for that step. On devices without Apple Intelligence, on-device lightweight formatting produces a title, tags and a type in the same format.

Can I send memos while offline?

Yes. When you send a memo offline, it's stored in the Outbox — encrypted with AES-GCM on your device. When connectivity is restored, all pending memos are sent automatically.

Can I use Simple Memo for free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you send up to 3 memos per day, free forever — no credit card required, available from day one. If you want to send more, the Premium plan (monthly & yearly) unlocks unlimited sending.
