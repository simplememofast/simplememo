Obsidian pricing: how far the free plan goes

Obsidian’s core app is free for personal and commercial use. Optional Sync comes in Standard and Plus plans; Publish and support licenses are separate purchases. Compare annual totals, storage and shared-vault conditions using official prices checked September 9, 2026.

By AI Ataka · Published August 23, 2026 · Updated and official prices checked September 9, 2026

Is Obsidian free to use?

Yes. The core app is free, including commercial use. You do not need Sync, Publish or Simple Memo to create and edit notes in a local vault.

Sync is an optional official service for device syncing; Publish is an optional web publishing service. Catalyst and commercial licenses support development. Official pricing and free-use conditions

Obsidian, syncing, and Simple Memo have separate costs

Choose a sync method that supports your operating systems and vault location. Besides Obsidian Sync, iCloud is an option between Apple devices; the official guide lists caveats when Windows is involved. Sync methods by device

Simple Memo is a separate app. Its purchase does not include Obsidian Sync or replace cross-device syncing. Select a destination in supported integration settings and verify that the first note reaches the intended file. See Simple Memo’s pricing FAQ for its own free allowance and paid plans.

Discounts and refunds

The official pricing page offers a 40% Sync and Publish discount for students, faculty and nonprofit employees; check application and eligibility conditions before purchase. Sync and Publish offer full refunds within seven days of purchase. Catalyst, commercial licenses and Obsidian Credit are non-refundable.

Storage is combined across the account’s vaults and includes attachments and version history. The 1 GB allowance is not reserved for text alone. Extra Plus storage is an upgrade; 100 GB is not included in the base $8 annual-equivalent / $10 monthly price. Official plans and storage limits

Older attachment versions are retained for two weeks on either plan. This differs from the one-month / twelve-month note history. Official history retention periods

Check your vault count, current total size, and largest PDF or image first. Standard is worth considering for one vault with files within 5 MB; compare Plus if you need multiple vaults or larger attachments. Anticipated future growth alone does not require starting with the higher plan.

Collaboration and backup conditions

Every collaborator needs an active Sync subscription. A vault you join does not count toward your own vault limit. Same-file live editing and shared cursors are not supported; changes are merged when synced. Shared-vault conditions

Shared vaults do not offer fine-grained read/edit permissions. Their purpose also differs from choosing pages for Publish. Before collaborating, check that the vault contains only material you intend to share.

Sync propagates changes across devices, including possible accidental deletions. Check the history window and keep an independent backup of important notes. Obsidian backup guidance

Frequently asked questions

Is Obsidian free to use?
Yes. The core app is free for personal and commercial use. Local note-taking needs no sign-up or Sync subscription. A commercial license is optional support for development.
Does annual Obsidian Sync charge me $4 each month?
No. Standard’s $4 is the monthly equivalent of an annual plan, totaling $48 a year. Monthly billing costs $5 a month. Plus is $8 equivalent / $96 a year, or $10 with monthly billing. These are official USD prices per user.
How do Standard and Plus differ?
Standard includes one vault, 1 GB total, 5 MB per file and one month of note history. Plus includes ten vaults, 10 GB total, 200 MB per file and twelve months of note history. Older attachment versions are kept for two weeks on both plans. Attachments and history count toward storage; upgrading Plus to as much as 100 GB is extra.
How much does Obsidian Publish cost?
Publish costs $8 per site per month equivalent with annual billing, totaling $96 a year, or $10 with monthly billing. It publishes notes on the web and is separate from Sync.
Can everyone use a shared vault on one person’s subscription?
No. Each collaborator needs an active Sync subscription. A vault you join does not count toward your own vault limit. Same-file live collaborative editing is not supported.
Are discounts or refunds available?
The official pricing page offers a 40% Sync and Publish discount for students, faculty and nonprofit employees. Sync and Publish qualify for a full refund within seven days of purchase. Catalyst, commercial licenses and Obsidian Credit are non-refundable.
Do I need Catalyst to unlock note-taking features?
Catalyst is not required for basic note-taking in the public app. The listed $25 one-time purchase supports development and provides benefits such as early access to beta builds.

Scope of this comparison
