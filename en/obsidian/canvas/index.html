Place sentences, refer to notes, connect with lines

Canvas is the core plug-in of Obsidian that places text cards and notebook cards on a flat surface. First, line up only three pieces, connect two pieces with a line, and leave the relationship on the label. Make sure that the text and the notes to be referenced are also saved.

New creationPlace the cardLines and labelsMove the cardSave and redisplayPractice file

The text on the card and the reference memo are short sentences created by the author. It is not a record of the actual user. The arrangement of the diagrams is manually arranged and is not the result of measuring the correctness of knowledge or productivity.

1. Create an empty Canvas from the command palette

In this environment, Canvas and command palette have been enabled. Open the command palette in Ctrl＋P and select Canvas: Create new canvas. The name in the file list on the left was Untitled.canvas. The original file saved in an empty state is {}'s 2 bytes.

The text of the card to be placed from now on is a guide to fictitious observation and confirmation. The 資料/参照メモ.md for reference was prepared before the app was launched. The operation of adding cards is done on the actual Canvas.

2. Put two short sentences and one existing notebook

Double-click the blank space of Canvas and enter text. I did the same operation in another blank area and made two text cards. The saved full text is as follows.

Text Card A

# 観察カード

著者作成の短文を並べます。

Text Card B

# 次の確認

参照メモを開いて本文を確認します。

Next, drag 資料/参照メモ.md in the file list to Canvas. The third one is a file card that refers to the Markdown. In this saved data, two were type: text and one was type: file.

The height of the short sentence card is 60, and the full text is not displayed at once. There is a scroll bar in the image card, and the visible part is different. The full text is not judged only by the display on the card, and the text and saved contents are also checked.

3. Connect the two cards and label the line “本文を確認”

  1. Place the pointer on the right edge of the observation card and drag it from the circle that appears to the left edge of the next confirmation card.
  2. Double-click the line you created.
  3. Enter 本文を確認, then press Esc to finish editing the label.

The saved connection goes from the right side of the observation card to the left side of the next confirmation card, and the label is 本文を確認. The third reference memo does not connect lines in this example. In the image, you can also see the label between the two sheets and the body of the reference note.

4. Select the card and drag the inside to change the position

I selected the reference note’s file card and dragged its interior to move it. On the first attempt, I grabbed its edge and accidentally resized it. After recording that state, I undid the change with Ctrl＋Z and dragged from inside the card again.

In the final saved data, only the coordinates of the file card changed from x=-32, y=61 to x=-160, y=-60, and the size, reference destination, and ID of 400×400 were the same. The meaning of the connection is the same as the other two texts.

This is a record of confirming an internal drag. It is not an operation that completely verifies the function of resizing. Coordinates are comparisons of stored contents, not values that indicate time required or efficiency.

5. Save and reopen Canvas from another note

I saved it with Ctrl＋S, opened another Markdown from the file list, and then returned to Untitled.canvas. We are checking the saved three cards, one connection, and label.

The original Canvas file remained 612 bytes with the same SHA256 after moving it, reopening it, and quitting the app. However, the Canvas viewport and the scroll position inside the text card changed. Matching saved files and an identical on-screen view are separate things to verify.

This time, I reopened it within the process of the same app. I have not tried to restore, another device, or synchronize after closing and restarting the entire app. Finally, the app/installer 1.13.7 was displayed in the General settings, and my app and display process ended normally.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Canvas text card and the notebook card?
The text of this text card is saved in the text field in .canvas, and the note card refers to 資料/参照メモ.md in the file field. I have not tried the operation of converting the text card to another Markdown or the backlink operation.
How to connect cards to each other and name the lines?
Place the pointer on a card’s edge and drag the circle that appears to another card. Double-click the line, enter a label, and press Esc to finish editing. In this example, we connected two text cards and saved the label 本文を確認.
Do I also need a reference note for the downloaded Canvas?
The file card in this example refers to the relative path 資料/参照メモ.md. Alongside Untitled.canvas, place 参照メモ.md in the 資料 folder within the same vault. A ZIP preserves the original relative paths of both files. Importing them on a reader’s device or into another vault has not been tested.
If I reopen the saved Canvas, will the screen be exactly the same?
This time, when I moved to another note in the same app and reopened Canvas, the saved 612 bytes and SHA256 matched. On the other hand, the display position of the screen and the scrolling position in the text card have changed. After restarting the entire app, another terminal, synchronization, speed and productivity are not checked.
