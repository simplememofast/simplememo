Simple Memo for Freelancers
Keep Everything in Your Inbox

Client change requests, invoice deadlines, post-meeting follow-ups — freelancers juggle multiple projects constantly. Simple Memo launches in 0.4s (warm launch, iPhone 16e) and sends with one tap, so you can send each task to your inbox as soon as it comes up.

The Freelancer Task Management Problem

Freelancers can't rely on a shared company project management system. Every time you open Notion or Trello, there's the friction of "which board was that?" Verbal change requests during phone calls go unrecorded, leading to misalignment with clients later. For freelancers, missed tasks don't just hurt productivity — they damage client trust.

Can't Capture Verbal Requests During Calls
Client says "oh, and could you also handle X" mid-call. There's no time to find the right app. That request evaporates from memory within minutes.
Forgotten Invoices Hurt Cash Flow
"I'll send the invoice at month end" — then the next project demands attention and it slips. Missed invoices are a serious cash flow problem for freelancers.
Too Many Tools, Too Much Overhead
Switching between Notion, Trello, Slack, calendar, and a notebook consumes real time. Freelancers need to minimize time spent managing tools.

The Inbox-as-Task-Manager Approach

What's the one tool freelancers check every single day without fail? Their email inbox. With Simple Memo, you send each task there. Gmail's snooze for To-Do, stars for priority flags, folders and labels for client organization — it enhances your existing workflow without replacing it. Your inbox stays the same, so there's little new to learn.

4-Step Freelancer Workflow

  1. Capture during client calls and meetings — The moment a client mentions a change request or new requirement, launch Simple Memo. "[ClientA] Color change: blue→green ← fix in next revision." Sent before the conversation moves on.
  2. Manage inbox memos as tasks — Snooze arrived memo emails in Gmail for the right date and time. Star high-priority items. Apply client labels automatically with Gmail filters.
  3. Capture project ideas and insights instantly — During commutes, in the shower, before bed — inspiration doesn't wait. Memos you send while offline are stored securely and auto-sent when connectivity returns.
  4. Use as invoice and follow-up reminders — Send "[ClientB] Invoice due March 31" and "[ClientC] Follow up 1 week after proposal" → snooze in Gmail. Stay on top of billing cycles.

Real Scenario Examples

Scenario 1 — Verbal Request During a Call
On a call with Client A, they say "Oh, and could you also change the CTA button copy from 'Sign Up Now' to 'Try for Free'?" Still talking, capture it immediately:
[ClientA LP] CTA copy: "Sign Up Now" → "Try for Free" ← bill as additional scope
Sent. After the call, Gmail auto-labels it "ClientA." Snooze it for the next working session. The change request is documented and the billing note is already there.
Scenario 2 — Invoice Reminder
Project delivered. Set the invoice reminder immediately before moving on to the next thing:
[ClientB] Invoice due March 31 — $2,400 ← bank: Chase ****1234
Snooze the email for March 29. Reminded on the day, invoice sent. Stay on top of billing cycles.
Scenario 3 — Late-Night Idea Capture
11pm, a strong proposal idea for Client C surfaces. No energy to open Notion. Pick up the phone and launch Simple Memo:
[ClientC Proposal] A/B test idea: replace hero image with video → competitor D reported +23% CV rate with same approach. Worth testing.
Send and sleep. Morning inbox has the idea waiting. Copy-paste into Notion's "ClientC Proposals" page and build it into the deck.
Scenario 4 — Post-Zoom Action Items
Zoom meeting just ended. Capture all action items before closing the laptop:
[ClientD MTG 3/5] Actions: ①Competitor research (me, by 3/12) ②3 design concepts (me, by 3/19) ③Approval workflow confirmation (client, by 3/10). Next MTG: 3/20 3pm
No need to rewatch the recording. The email record means "what did we decide in that meeting?" can be answered in seconds — and if the client needs it, you can forward that email.

FAQ

How do I manage tasks for multiple clients without mixing them up?

Start each memo with a client tag (e.g., [ClientA], [ClientB]). Your email's search instantly filters by client. Set up Gmail filters to auto-label incoming memos for an automatically organized inbox.

Can I use it as an invoice reminder?

Yes. Send a memo like "[ClientA] Invoice due March 31 ← don't forget" then snooze the email in Gmail to resurface on March 29. It only takes a moment.

Is it practical to take notes on a phone during a Zoom call?

Yes. Launch to send takes only seconds, so it doesn't interrupt the flow. During video calls, stepping off camera to jot a note is completely natural — that's the perfect moment for a quick Simple Memo capture.

How does it integrate with Notion, Asana, or other project management tools?

Use Zapier or Make to build a workflow: "memo email arrives → automatically add as task in Notion or Asana." Simple Memo is the fast capture layer; your project management tool handles structured organization. The two work in complementary layers.

Is it secure enough for client-related information?

Offline memos are stored with AES-GCM encryption on your device, and memo content is not persistently stored on Simple Memo's servers. After sending, security depends on your email provider. For highly confidential information, use your firm's secure communication tools. Simple Memo is best for quick operational notes and reminders.
