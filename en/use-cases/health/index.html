Use Case Guide for Health Tracking

Health & Diet Tracking Notes
Weight, Meals & Exercise Log

Morning weight, lunch details, gym workout volume, notable symptoms. The foundation of health management is consistent logging. With Simple Memo, record your weight in 10 seconds right after stepping off the scale. No complex input forms needed.

By AI ATAKA

Sound Familiar?

Health apps are too complex to maintain. You step on the scale but forget to log the number. You start a diet journal but give up in 3 days. The biggest enemy of health tracking is friction.

Health Apps Don't Stick
Graphs, calorie counting, barcode scanning... too many features make daily input feel like a chore.
Can't Recall Health Changes Later
"When did the headaches start?" "What did you eat last week?" — questions at the doctor you can't answer.
Diet Logging Lasts 3 Days
The more detailed you try to be, the less likely you are to continue. Simple logging sustains the habit.

Why Simple Memo Changes Health Tracking

Health logging with Simple Memo takes 10 seconds per entry. See the scale number, memo, send. For meals, just the dish name. For exercise, just the sets. This "minimum viable record" is the key to consistency. Since everything accumulates in email, you can see weight trends when reviewing a month later.

4-Step Health Tracking Workflow

  1. Morning: Log weight instantly — Step off the scale, open Simple Memo. "Weight: 68.2kg." Send. Done in 10 seconds.
  2. After meals: Quick food note — "Lunch: chicken salad bento, miso soup." No calorie counting needed.
  3. Post-workout: Training record — "Gym: bench press 60kg x8 x3, run 20min." Sets and time are enough.
  4. Weekend review — Review the week's emails to spot weight trends and eating patterns. Add reflections if needed.

Real Scenario Examples

Scenario 1 — Morning Weight Log
Step on the scale: 150.2 lbs. Quick memo:
[Weight] 3/6 150.2 lbs (-0.6 from yesterday) Before breakfast. Feeling good. Slept 7h
Scenario 2 — Meal Log
Quick note after lunch:
[Meals] 3/6 Lunch: grilled chicken salad, brown rice ball, veggie soup. No snacks. 1L water
Scenario 3 — Symptom Tracking
Mild headache starts in the afternoon. Record for future doctor visits:
[Health] 3/6 ~2pm mild headache. Only 5h sleep last night. Skipped breakfast. Low pressure front? Took ibuprofen → better by 4pm

FAQ

Can I create graphs from my data?

Simple Memo doesn't have built-in graphing, but you can copy email data to a spreadsheet for easy visualization. The key is using a consistent format each day.

How do I make daily logging a habit?

Each entry takes under 10 seconds. Pair it with an existing habit — like right after stepping on the scale each morning — and it becomes routine within 3 days.

Can I show health records to my doctor?

Yes. Search your email during the appointment to show symptom history in chronological order on your phone.

Is my health data private?

Memos are stored with AES-GCM encryption in the app. After sending, your email account's security applies. Data is only sent to your own email — never shared with third parties.

Is the free plan enough for health tracking?

The free plan allows 3 sends per day, free forever — morning weight, lunch log, and evening workout. That covers most people's needs.
