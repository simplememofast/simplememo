Simple Memo for Managers
Meetings, 1-on-1s & Decisions

A task you must follow up on between back-to-back meetings. A candid comment from a direct report during a 1-on-1. A key decision from the executive meeting. Simple Memo captures it all in 0.4s and sends to your inbox with one tap.

By AI ATAKA

Sound Familiar?

A manager's work is a continuous stream of decisions and communications. You thought you'd remember the meeting outcome, but it's fuzzy by morning. You planned to document 1-on-1 feedback, but the next meeting started. You don't even have time to open your task manager. The core problem: there's simply no time to write things down.

Back-to-Back Meetings Leave Gaps
When meetings run 30 minutes apart, there's zero time to process the previous one. Memory fades fast.
1-on-1 Feedback Never Gets Documented
Without documented growth records and feedback, you can't cite specifics during performance reviews.
Decision Rationale Gets Lost
When asked "why did we decide that?" months later, you can't recall the context behind the decision.

Why Simple Memo Solves the Manager's Note Problem

Simple Memo's strength is that it works in the 30-second gap between meetings. Launch, type, send — all one-handed. Memos arrive as emails you can search, forward, or convert to tasks later. No complex setup like dedicated meeting-notes apps.

4-Step Manager Workflow

  1. During meetings: Capture decisions & actions instantly — Note what was decided, who owns it, and the deadline. E.g., "[Sales MTG] Q3 target raised to 120%. Tanaka to prepare deck by Friday."
  2. One-tap send to your email — Tap send. Email arrives withwith one tap. Done before you walk to the next meeting room.
  3. After 1-on-1s: Record feedback immediately — Right after the session, memo "[1on1/Sato] Great proposal on presentation. Next: work on slide structure." Feedback history accumulates naturally.
  4. Weekly review: Prioritize next week — Review the week's memo emails in one view. Carry forward incomplete tasks and check progress on decisions.

Real Scenario Examples

Scenario 1 — After an Executive Meeting
The exec meeting just ended. 5 minutes until the next team standup. In the elevator, you launch Simple Memo:
[Exec MTG] Budget cut 15% next FY. Hiring freeze until April. Cloud migration continues as priority. → Share at team standup
Sent. You can open this email during the team meeting for accurate information sharing.
Scenario 2 — 1-on-1 Feedback
Walking back from a 1-on-1 with a direct report:
[1on1/Yamada] Strong leadership showing. Delegation still weak. Asked to create delegation list by next session.
Search "Yamada" before the annual review, and every feedback note appears chronologically.
Scenario 3 — Snap Decision Documentation
A client calls requesting an immediate price decision. After agreeing, you record immediately:
[Client A] Approved 10% discount. Conditions: annual contract + prepayment. Rationale: LTV calc shows positive ROI. → Request contract revision

FAQ

Won't taking notes during meetings break my focus?

Simple Memo launches in 0.4s and sends with one tap — you're back to the discussion in under 10 seconds. Just jot down the key decision or action item, not full sentences.

Can I use it to track feedback for direct reports?

Absolutely. After each 1-on-1, send a quick memo tagged with the person's name. Before performance reviews, search their name and you'll have a chronological feedback history ready.

Is it safe for confidential business information?

Memos are stored with AES-GCM encryption in the app. Once sent, your organization's email security policies apply. Use it within your company's approved email environment.

How do I manage notes across multiple projects?

Prefix each memo with a project tag like "[Hiring PJ]" or "[Product Launch]". Set up Gmail filters to auto-label by keyword for instant project-based filtering.

Is the free plan enough for a busy manager?

The free plan allows 3 sends per day, free forever. For managers with multiple daily meetings, the Premium plan (500 yen/month, unlimited sends) is recommended. Start with the Free plan to see if it fits your workflow.
