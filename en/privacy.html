Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

YURIKA, K.K. ("we," "us," or "our"), a company established in Japan, explains in this Policy how we handle personal information in the iOS app "Simple Memo - Obsidian Voice" (listed as "Obsidian連携音声シンプルメモ" on the App Store in Japan and some other countries and regions) and on our website simplememofast.com (together, the "Service"). Our company details and contact information are in Section 11.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

(A) Information you provide

  • Recipient email address ("To")
  • Subject and signature settings
  • Memo content (to the extent necessary to deliver it to the destination you choose)
  • If you connect Notion: your authorization for our server to create pages in the Notion workspace you choose (see (I))
  • If you use our contact form, email us or answer our roadmap survey: what you write and any contact details you give (see Section 14)

(B) Device and usage information

  • Basic device and OS information
  • Operational logs (send timestamps, status)
  • Usage events (screen views, interactions, and send outcomes), a random device-level identifier, session IDs, send IDs, and diagnostics such as whether app launch completed
  • IP address and request information, which our server receives with every request and which our hosting provider records in server logs for security, abuse prevention, rate limiting and troubleshooting (see (J))

Usage events do not contain email addresses or memo text, but a send ID can link them to the recipient email hash in our delivery records. We therefore treat these events, device identifiers, and diagnostics as data that can be linked to the user. Section 12 describes where the device identifier is stored and what happens to it after the app is deleted.

(C) Subscription and Refund Notifications From Apple

To understand subscription operations and billing failures, we verify signed notifications from Apple and record subscription starts, renewals, changes to automatic renewal, failed renewals, expirations, refunds, and related events. Stored fields include notification and transaction identifiers, subscription product identifiers, notification types and subtypes, production or test environment, dates such as purchase, expiration, revocation, signing and receipt, and revocation reasons supplied by Apple. These Apple identifiers are not treated as fully anonymous information. These notification records contain no email addresses, memo content or installation identifiers. We do not retain a mapping from Apple transaction identifiers to installation identifiers or recipient email addresses. Section (D) describes matching notifications during processing to create separate analytics records. We cannot locate the original notification records from an email-address-only deletion request.

(D) Subscription State and Retention Analytics

When this analytics feature is enabled in the app, we receive signed purchase and renewal information from Apple through the device, verify it, and store subscription facts with an installation identifier for comparison with usage-retention events. Recorded fields include the subscription product, purchased or family-shared ownership, price and currency, dates such as purchase, expiration, revocation, signing, observation and receipt, automatic-renewal status, billing retry, grace period, expiration reason, production or test environment, and the app's runtime environment. During processing, Apple transaction identifiers are used to match the notifications described in (C); counts and latest dates by notification type available at that time are also recorded. Apple transaction identifiers and raw signed information are not retained alongside these analytics records. The records also exclude email addresses and memo content, but installation identifiers, products, dates and prices can be linked to usage information and are not treated as anonymous. (E) shows which app versions send this information and whether you can choose.

Scheduled cleanup deletes these analytics records more than 90 days after receipt. Deletion through the app removes records associated with the deletion information retained on that device and rejects delayed uploads under the erased information. This does not collectively delete the original Apple notification records in (C), another device's records, or records held by external analytics services. We may be unable to identify the affected records if, for example, the device's deletion information has been lost.

(G) Email address verification

To let you send memos to an email address, we verify that you control it, using a 6-digit code, a link in an email, or Sign in with Apple or Google ((H)).

  • Our server (Cloudflare Workers KV) keeps your email address in readable form in these verification records:
    • the pending code record, with the code, for 10 minutes;
    • the record that an email link has been used, for 30 minutes;
    • the verified-address record for each iPhone, which authorizes sending, for 365 days after verification (verifying again starts a new 365 days). Deleting your account removes the record for the iPhone you delete from (see Section 15).
  • Our database (Cloudflare D1) keeps a shortened SHA-256 hash of your address with send IDs (35 days) and with registration and reminder records (see Sections 5 and 9). A hash like this is pseudonymous, not anonymous: anyone who already knows an address can calculate the same value, so we treat it as personal data.
  • When you request a code, complete verification, verify with Apple or Google, or send a memo by email, we register your address as a contact at Resend, our email delivery provider (Section 5(E)). The contact is marked as unsubscribed unless you turn on reminders. This happens whether or not you turn on reminders, and also for addresses that request a code but do not complete verification.
  • If you request a code but do not complete verification, we may send up to three emails, about 6, 24 and 72 hours later, asking you to finish. Each has an unsubscribe link.

(H) Sign in with Apple or Google

You can fill in and verify your email address with Sign in with Apple or with your Google account. In both cases the app signs in to Firebase Authentication (Google LLC), which processes data only in data centers in the United States.

  • Sign in with Apple: the app asks Apple for your name and email address. Apple shares your name only the first time you sign in, and on Apple's screen you can edit your name or choose to hide your email address. The app passes your name to Firebase, which uses it as the account's display name.
  • Google: Google provides your email address and the basic profile information that comes with your Google account.
  • Firebase keeps this sign-in record (email address, name if provided, and provider and user identifiers) until we delete it. Firebase keeps IP addresses used for sign-in for a few weeks.
  • Deleting your account in the app does not currently delete the Firebase record or end the app's link with your Apple Account. Email us (Section 11) and we will delete the Firebase record. You can stop using Sign in with Apple for this app at any time in your Apple Account settings.
  • We use the email address from these sign-ins to verify your address on our server ((G)).

(I) Notion

If you connect Notion (version 5.8.12 and later):

  • Notion's authorization for our server (OAuth tokens) is kept encrypted on our server (Cloudflare D1), together with the IDs of the Inbox database created in your workspace. The tokens are never stored on your iPhone.
  • When you save a memo to Notion, the memo text, a title made from it, the time and a capture ID pass through our server (Cloudflare Workers) to Notion's API. We do not store the memo text. We keep a delivery receipt (a cryptographic code calculated from the content, and the Notion page ID) to avoid creating duplicate pages. Receipts for completed saves are deleted after 30 days.
  • "Disconnect" in Settings revokes our access at Notion and deletes the stored tokens. The Inbox setup and recent receipts are kept so that reconnecting does not create duplicates. Deleting your account in the app deletes the Notion connection records created from that iPhone.
  • Pages you save are kept by Notion Labs, Inc. (United States) in your workspace under Notion's own terms and privacy policy. Delete them in Notion.

(J) Server logs

Our API runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc., United States). Cloudflare Workers Logs records each request for 7 days. The records include the IP address; the approximate location that Cloudflare derives from the IP address (country, region, city, postal code, and latitude and longitude); the network (ASN); browser or app information (user agent); the requested URL, including query parameters; other request metadata; and, for messages sent to our support address, the sender and recipient addresses. We use these logs for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. They may be stored in Cloudflare data centers outside your country. Separately, for rate limiting, our server keeps a shortened hash of your IP address for up to 48 hours.

4. Handling of Memo Content (Important)

  • Memo content is sent to the destination you choose, based on your action. Saving to Obsidian, Reminders or Calendar is done on your iPhone and does not pass through our server.
  • Email: the memo content and recipient address pass through our server (Cloudflare Workers) and our email delivery provider Resend. We do not store memo content on our servers. Resend stores each email (including the memo content, subject and recipient address) and its delivery logs in the United States for 30 days. We cannot delete individual emails at Resend through its API.
  • Notion: the memo content passes through our server to Notion (1(I)).
  • Voice input: on iPhone (iOS 26 or later), speech is transcribed on the device. The app does not create recordings and does not send audio to us. Dictation through the keyboard, Apple Watch or Siri is handled by Apple.
  • On your iPhone, sent history (the latest 100 items), unsent memos and drafts are stored encrypted (Section 12). They stay until you delete them or the app. Deleting your account does not delete them.
  • Until August 25, 2026, delivery-event records that our server kept with reminder records could include the recipient address and the email subject. These records are deleted 180 days after receipt.
  • We do not intend to view memo content except where necessary for legitimate purposes such as legal compliance, abuse prevention, or troubleshooting.

5. Email Reminder Feature (Optional) and Other Emails

To help you build a memo-writing habit, we offer an optional reminder email feature for users who explicitly opt in.

(A) Consent

  • This feature is disabled by default. We send reminder emails only after you explicitly enable it on the verification screen or in Settings.
  • If you have not enabled reminders, we may send one email asking whether you would like to receive them, once your email address is verified and you have not sent a memo for a while (roughly 72 hours after your last send). We send no reminders unless you choose to receive them.
  • You can disable it at any time from Settings (one tap), or from the unsubscribe link in any reminder email.
  • Emails about unfinished verification are described in 1(G).

(B) Information We Store

  • A shortened SHA-256 hash of your email address, stored on our servers (Cloudflare D1)
  • Your consent state (enabled/disabled), last memo send timestamp, and total send count
  • Reminder send history (timestamps for sent/delivered/opened/clicked/bounced events)
  • Your email address in readable form: in the contact list at Resend and in our server's verification records. These exist whether or not you turn on reminders (see 1(G)).

(C) Sending Frequency

  • Reminders are sent at most once per stall window: 24 hours, 72 hours, and 7 days after your last memo send.
  • Per-user cap: max 2 reminders per 7 days. Service-wide cap: max 80 reminders per day.

(D) Automatic Opt-out

  • If a reminder email bounces or generates a complaint, we immediately stop sending to that address and disable the consent flag.

(E) Email delivery provider

Emails sent by our server, including memos you send, verification emails and reminders, are delivered by Resend, which is operated by Plus Five Five, Inc. (United States), under its Data Processing Addendum. Resend also keeps our contact list. Resend stores email content, metadata and delivery logs in the United States for 30 days (see Section 4).

12. Technical notes on encryption (for reference)

To protect user privacy, the Service implements the following technical measures. These are oriented toward "data minimization" and "on-device protection" rather than promising blanket security.

  • On-device storage: The offline pending-send queue (Outbox) and send history are encrypted on-device with AES-GCM-256 via Apple's native CryptoKit, and stored with file protection class completeFileProtectionUntilFirstUserAuthentication.
  • Key management: A 256-bit symmetric key is stored in Apple Keychain Services (kSecClassGenericPassword) with protection that allows access after the first unlock following a restart and prevents migration to another device. The app reads the key for encryption and decryption. Key copies included in backups are protected by a device-specific key and cannot be used on another device (Apple's explanation). Before changing devices, confirm that important memos have reached their destination, such as your mailbox or Obsidian vault.
  • Transport: API communication uses TLS (HTTPS). The memo-sending URLSessionConfiguration.ephemeral avoids persisting that session’s caches, cookies, and credentials to disk. This is separate from analytics storage and transmission, and from external SDK networking.
  • Email body handling: Memo bodies transit through our Relay API (Cloudflare Workers) and the external delivery infrastructure (Resend) for the sole purpose of sending email. We do not persistently store them on our infrastructure, and they are not written to our logs (except in DEBUG builds during local development). Resend keeps sent emails for 30 days (Section 4).
  • Email address storage on the server: Cloudflare D1 stores a mapping between send IDs and shortened SHA-256 hashes of recipient addresses. These delivery records are created regardless of reminder opt-in. Our verification records in Cloudflare KV hold the address in readable form (1(G)), and Resend holds it as a contact (1(G), Section 5).
  • Dependencies on the data path: Memo body encryption (CryptoKit), key storage (Keychain Services), and network transport (URLSession / Network.framework) are all Apple-native — no third-party SDK sits in the data path.
  • Sign-in and abuse-prevention SDKs: Sign in with Apple uses Apple's AuthenticationServices and Firebase Authentication; filling in your address from a Google account uses GoogleSignIn-iOS and Firebase Authentication (1(H)). Firebase App Check initializes at launch and uses Apple App Attest to protect backend API access.
  • First-party usage analytics: We send events to our /v1/analytics/event endpoint to understand usage and improve quality. Fields include a random installation identifier, session identifier, event type, event identifier and time, app version and build, language, and allowed attributes such as send outcomes. The payload is designed to exclude raw email addresses, memo text, and verification codes, but send IDs can link events to recipient email hashes. This installation identifier is stored in the device's Keychain. Because of how iOS works, Keychain items may remain on the device after the app is deleted; in that case, the same identifier is used again after reinstalling. Versions 5.9.9 and earlier and 5.9.16 to 5.9.32 also set this identifier as AppsFlyer's Customer User ID (1(E)).
  • AppsFlyer measurement: In versions 5.9.9 and earlier and 5.9.16 to 5.9.32 (see 1(E) for when the SDK starts), the app sends events for first successful memo send, first successful capture, each successful memo save (email sends and saves to Obsidian or Notion), successful verification, paywall views, plan selection, and successful purchases. Fields set by the app include, where applicable, the input method (such as keyboard or voice), the sending path, where the memo was captured (iPhone app, Apple Watch, Siri Shortcuts, or the share sheet), the destination type (email, Obsidian, or Notion), the app language, the measurement-definition version, the build and runtime type, the purchase price and currency of verified transactions, the subscription period, the offer type, and the paywall version and entry point. We set the same installation identifier used in first-party analytics as AppsFlyer’s Customer User ID, allowing acquisition and usage records to be linked. Event fields set by our app do not directly include email addresses, memo text, or verification codes. The SDK itself also collects technical information, including the IP address, device model, OS and app version, the identifier for vendor (IDFV) and its own AppsFlyer ID, and uses Apple's SKAdNetwork attribution. Version 5.9.32 blocks sharing with all AppsFlyer integrated partners, does not let the SDK read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and turns off Apple Ads attribution; earlier versions did not apply these settings. iOS provides the IDFA only to apps you have allowed to track you, and this app asked for that permission only in version 5.9.15. The app receives AppsFlyer's attribution result and sends only a coarse category (for example, organic or non-organic, our website, or other) to our first-party analytics with the installation identifier. This is not an exhaustive list of technical information collected by the SDK. Identifiers and linkable records are not treated as fully anonymous information. Version 5.9.15 is described in 1(F).

Note: The Service is not End-to-End Encrypted (E2EE). Memo bodies are delivered through standard SMTP so you (the recipient) can read them in your normal mail client. For workloads requiring true E2EE, consider purpose-built services such as Standard Notes, Signal, or ProtonMail.

(B) Support email

  • Emails to support@simplememofast.com pass through Cloudflare Email Routing and our server, and are delivered to our support mailbox, provided by Google LLC (Gmail, United States). We have not yet set a fixed period for deleting them there.
  • Our server may also record the message text and a hash of your address in our database for 180 days. For emails from businesses, we may keep the sender address, display name and subject for 180 days.
  • Server logs record the sender and recipient addresses for 7 days (1(J)).

(C) Roadmap survey

  • The survey on our roadmap page stores your answers, any free text (up to 1,000 characters), your language, the page, and a shortened hash of your IP address. It stores your email address only if you tick the box, and uses it only to reply or to tell you when something ships.
  • Responses are stored in our database (Cloudflare D1). We have not yet set a deletion period.
  • Responses are not linked to your app account. To delete a response, email us from the address you entered. Responses without an email address cannot be linked to you.

15. Deleting Your Account and Data

In the app, go to Settings > Account > Delete Account.

Deletion removes, for the email address verified on that iPhone:

  • the verification records on our server for that iPhone, your reminder settings and history, registration records, delivery records, usage events linked through send IDs, and contact form and support email records linked to your address
  • your contact at Resend
  • the Notion connection records created from that iPhone (1(I))
  • the installation identifier on that iPhone, which is replaced with a new one

We keep, after deletion:

  • do-not-mail records (for example after a bounce, complaint or unsubscribe) and a record that the address was deleted (hashes and times), so that we do not email it again
  • original Apple notifications (1(C)) until their 180 days end, and usage events that cannot be linked to your address until their 90 days end
  • copies at our providers until their own periods end: emails at Resend (30 days), server logs (7 days) and database recovery copies (up to 30 days)

Deletion in the app does not reach:

  • memos, history and drafts on your iPhone (delete them, or delete the app)
  • the verified email address and the device token that the app keeps in your iPhone's Keychain (Keychain items can remain even after you delete the app)
  • verification records for the same address on another iPhone (delete your account on that iPhone too, or email us)
  • records for an address you requested a code for but did not verify, including its contact at Resend (email us)
  • the Firebase sign-in record (1(H)), data held by AppsFlyer, emails in our support mailbox, and roadmap survey responses (email us)

If you changed or cleared the recipient address before deleting, or the app could not finish, email us and we will delete the remaining records.

In versions 5.9.16 to 5.9.32, if you keep using the app after deletion, AppsFlyer measurement starts again with the new installation identifier (1(E)).

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

16. Your Choices and Rights

(A) Choices

  • Reminder emails: turn them off in Settings or use the unsubscribe link (Section 5).
  • Notion: disconnect in Settings (1(I)).
  • History: delete items in the app.
  • Analytics and AppsFlyer: see 1(E) for what your version allows.
  • Website: Google Analytics opt-out and cookie settings (13(A)); requests to AppsFlyer about clicks it already recorded (13(C)).

(B) Requests about your personal data

You can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or ask us to stop or restrict using it, by emailing support@simplememofast.com, preferably from the email address concerned. To protect your data, we may ask you to confirm that you control the address. We will respond within one month and, if we need more time, tell you why within that month.

(C) Complaints

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority:

  • In the EEA: the authority in the country where you live or work, or where you think the problem occurred (list of authorities).
  • In the United Kingdom: the Information Commission (formerly the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).
  • In Switzerland: the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (edoeb.admin.ch).
  • In Japan: the Personal Information Protection Commission (ppc.go.jp).

We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first (Section 11).
