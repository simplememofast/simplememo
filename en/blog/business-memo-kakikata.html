5 frameworks for faster work

What is a business memo - Why is "how to write" important

A business memo is a document for concisely recording and sharing work information. Meeting minutes, business meeting points, task reminders, fragments of ideas - all are included in business notes.

However, many business people are not aware of the difference between "taking notes" and "writing notes that can be used". Memos that only copy what you have heard will not lead to action even if you look back later.

3 features common to excellent business notes:

  • Actionable — "What to do next" is clear after reading
  • structured — Information is organized along the framework
  • Searchable — You can quickly find "that memo" at a later date

By contrast, a typical example of an unhelpful note is a jumble of scribbles. A note containing only keywords, with no date, time or context, will be no easier to understand than ciphertext after 1 week.

All of the 5 frameworks introduced in this article aim to be "memos that can be used from the moment you write them". There are no difficult techniques. Just by writing along the template, the quality of your notes will change dramatically.

Framework 1: 5W1H Memo - Organize the main points after the meeting

Framework 01
5W1H Memo
A framework that records the decisions of the meeting without omission with the 6 elements of Who, What, When, Where, Why, and How.

Immediately after the meeting, "What was decided in the end?" Do you ever think so? The most common failure in meeting memos is to become a "memo of speech". Even if you record who said what, it makes no sense if you don't know the next action.

5W1H Memo is a framework for recording the meeting's decisions and action items without omission.

■ Meeting: [Regular meeting / Project name] ■ Date and time: 2026-03-21 14:00-15:00 ■ Attendees: Tanaka, Sato, Suzuki [Decisions] 1. What: Set the new feature’s release date to 4-15 Who: Development team (Suzuki leads) When: 4-15 Why: To meet the Q2 sales target How: Complete the remaining tasks in sprint 3 [Action items] - [ ] Suzuki: Prepare the test plan by 3/25 - [ ] Tanaka: Tell the client the release date (3/22) - [ ] Sato: Prepare marketing materials by 4/1 [Pending] - Additional budget approval → Decide at the next meeting

5W1H Memo tips:

  • Record only decisions in real time during the meeting
  • Be sure to write person in charge and deadline on the action item
  • Write up the notes within 5 minutes of the meeting, while your memory is fresh.
  • Specify the pending items and ensure that they are connected to the next agenda

Framework 2: Action First — Conclusion → Background → Next Step

Framework 02
Action First
Write the conclusion (Action) first, and record it in the order of background (Background) and next steps (Next Steps). A memo technique that allows readers to grasp the main points in the first 1 line.

The most important principle in business memos is "conclusion first". As with reports and emails, by writing notes from the conclusion, readers (including your future self) can grasp the main points in an instant.

The action-first framework consists of only 3 elements:

[A: Action (conclusions and decisions)] → Resubmit the estimate to Company A by next Monday [B: Background (why did it happen)] - The other party pointed out that "15% higher than the competitor's estimate" - 10% discount is possible with the approval of the general manager - Policy to propose an additional delivery time reduction option [N: Next Step] 1. Create a quotation after the discount today 2. I'll get the manager's final confirmation tomorrow morning 3. Send an email to Company A and Director Tanaka at 10 on Monday

The strength of this framework is high versatility. It can be used not only for meeting memos, but also for all situations, such as reporting to the boss, handover memos, and task memos for yourself.

3 rules to make action first a habit:

  • Be sure to write "what to do" on the 1 line of the memo
  • The background fits within bullet points 3
  • The next step is numbered in time series

Framework 3: Golden Template for Business Meeting Memos

Framework 03
Business meeting memo (BANT + Next Action)
A template that reliably records BANT information (budget, approver, needs, time) and the next action in sales and business negotiations. Posting to CRM is also smooth.

Business meeting memos are a particularly important category among business memos, where "how to write". Because the quality of the business negotiation memo affects the order rate as it is.

There are no salespeople who do not take notes during business negotiations, but there are many people who do not systematize "what to write down". It is a pattern that you try to enter it into the CRM after returning to work and realize that "I forgot to ask the most important thing".

business negotiation memo is based on the BANT framework and adds information specific to business negotiations:

■ Business meeting: ○○ Co., Ltd. / Director Tanaka, Section Chief Sato ■ Date and time: 2026-03-21 10:00 ■ Location: Conference Room B [BANT Information] B (Budget): Annual budget 5000000 yen, remaining 1000000 yen for this term A (Authority): General Manager Tanaka is in charge of final approval, Chief Sato is in charge of selection N (Needs): Dissatisfied with the operability of the current system, especially mobile support T (Timeline): 6 I want to introduce it by the end of the month (in the next budget) [Competitive situation] - Company B's tools are currently being tried - Company B's price is cheaper, but I'm worried about the support system [The other party's temperature feeling] ★★★★☆ (4/5) - Director Tanaka: Positive I want to show the demonstration in the company," he said - Section Chief Sato: Be careful. I want to check if I can clear the security standards [Next Action] 1. Send the security white paper to 3/22 2. Adjustment of the schedule of the in-house demonstration in the week of 4 1 3. Submit the quotation by 3/25

Points of business meeting memo:

  • Record the temperature feeling at the 5 level (helpful when looking back on business negotiations later)
  • quote the other party's statement as it is ("I want to show the demo in-house", etc.)
  • Competition information must be recorded (required for proposal customization)
  • Be sure to enter date in the Next Action

Framework 4: 1 Line Memo → Clearing Workflow (Application of Captio Formula)

Framework 04
1 line capture → clear book flow
The moment you think of it, write down only the 1 line, and then clear it later 2 stage memo technique. " Reduce the spillage of ideas by lowering the hurdle of writing to the extreme.

Ideas and insights at work do not arise only while you are sitting at your desk. While traveling, in an elevator, or after lunch—ideas that come to mind unexpectedly are often especially valuable.

However, when I tried to write a "proper note" at that moment, it took too long and I didn't write it in the end. This is the biggest problem.

The solution to this problem is "1 line memo → clear book workflow". Originally, it was a method inspired by the idea of "mail addressed to yourself" of the Captio app, and separates notes into the 2 stage of capture (capture) and processing (organization).

Step 1: Capture (time required: seconds)

Add a case to Company A's proposal Next week's 1on1 talk about the evaluation system The CVR of the adopted LP is low → First view improvement? Expense settlement deadline 3/25

The point is that you don't have to write a sentence. Only keywords, stop body language is OK. The important thing is the fact that I "wrote".

Step 2: Clearing book (Time required: 1 day 1 times, 15 minutes)

1 At the end of the day, or the next morning, look back at the accumulated 1 line memo and clear it:

[Original memo] Add a case to Company A's proposal → [Seisho] Add last month's company B's introduction case to the p.8 of Company A's proposal. Check the figures with Mr. Yamada of the Sales Department. Deadline: 3/24. [Original memo] Talk about the evaluation system in next week's 1on1 → [Seisho] Add to 1on1 agenda: ・Confirmation of the manager's policy on the self-evaluation of Q1 ・Confirmation of the target setting for the second half of the year

1 line memo → Advantages of clear book:

  • The psychological hurdle of the memo is dramatically lowered(1 just write a line)
  • Decrease the number of ideas spilling
  • In the process of clearing, thoughts are organized
  • Separating capture from thinking improves the quality of both.

This workflow is fully consistent with the design philosophy of SimpleMemo's "0.4 second start (iPhone 16e, actual measurement of warm start) → instant memo → email transmission" The faster the capture speed, the less spills there will be.

Framework 5: Daily Report Memo (3 Daily Report)

Framework 05
3 Daily Report
An ultra-lightweight framework that completes the daily report with only the 3 lines of "what I did", "aware" and "what to do tomorrow". Make it a habit to look back every day.

Daily reports have become a "cumbersome obligation" for many business people. Formal work to submit to the boss - isn't that how most people feel?

However, if you use the daily report as a "memo for yourself", it will be useful to look back on your work. The problem is that the traditional daily report is "too long".

3 Daily Report is a framework that completes the daily report with only one 3 item:

[2026-03-21 (Friday)] 1. What I did: Completed the proposal of Company A and had my first business meeting with Company B (temperature sense ★★★☆☆) 2. Notice: Company B emphasizes the support system over price 3. Things to do tomorrow: Prepare support system materials for company B, follow-up call company C

Is this all? You may think so. However, by condensing it into this 3 line, several important effects are created:

  • The hurdle for writing is extremely low — 3 lines can be written in 2 minutes. That's why I can continue every day
  • "Awareness" is accumulated - 1 If you look back at the "awareness" for a month, you can see the pattern
  • You can automatically prepare for the next day — Start the morning faster by writing "To do tomorrow"
  • It will be easier to review weekly and monthly - You can list the results during the period just by looking back at the daily report

3 Tips for continuing the daily report:

  • Write every day at the same time (e.g. 5 minutes before the end of work)
  • Don't aim for perfection——3 line only
  • "Notice" is OK even if emotion and discomfort (such as "today's meeting was too long")
  • Take time to look back at 5 days on the weekend

Digital vs Handwriting - Distinguishing Business Memos

"Which is better, digital or handwritten business notes?" - This is an eternal debate, but the correct answer is " use it according to the situation".

Item Digital Memo Handwritten memo
SpeedPeople who type fast are overwhelmingDrawings and rough sketches are fast by handwriting
SearchabilityFor a moment in the full text searchNeed to search visually
Memory consolidationSlightly weakIt is easy to remember by moving your hands
ShareCopy and paste to share immediatelyThe trouble of taking pictures occurs
Organize and editEasy to sort and addA clear book is often required
Properness of the sceneMeetings, business negotiations, daily reportsBreast, Mind Map, Learning

Recommended use:

  • Conference Memo → Digital: Action items can be transferred to task management as they are
  • Business meeting memo → Digital: Smooth transfer to CRM. You can check it on your smartphone while moving
  • Brest → Handwriting: Paper and pen are ideal for free ideas
  • 1 Line Memo → Digital (Smartphone): Instant capture anytime, anywhere
  • Daily report → Digital: Accumulate and use it for search and review

The important thing is that handwritten notes are eventually aggregated digitally. Handwritten breast notes are photographed and stored digitally, and the book is done digitally - this hybrid operation of " handwritten capture → digital calligraphy" is the most efficient memo technique for modern business people.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I write business notes in bullet points or sentences?

Basically, we recommend bullet points. The purpose of business notes is to "convey information quickly and lead to action". If you write in writing, it will take a long time to read, and the main points will be buried. However, let's record the other party's statements and nuances important information in short sentences.

I don't have time to take notes. What are the tips for taking notes during the meeting?

The trick is not to try to write everything. During the meeting, please record only decisions and action items (who, what, by when). There is no need to record the statement word by word. In addition, it is also effective to use the "1 line memo" method of the framework 4, to write only the keywords in bullet points and write them in 5 minutes after the meeting.

Which should I use in business, a paper notepad or a smartphone app?

In conclusion, it is best to use it differently in situations. Digital is advantageous for "search and share later" memos such as meetings, business negotiations, and daily reports. Handwriting is effective in situations where you "think freely", such as breast and mind maps. The important thing is that handwritten notes will eventually be aggregated digitally. Let's establish a flow of handwriting → photo → digital calligraphy.

Do I need to remember all the 5 frameworks?

No, It's enough to start with 1. First of all, we recommend that you start with "3 Daily Report". Because you can write 2 minutes every day, it is easy to continue, and you will acquire the habit of looking back. Once you get used to it, let's gradually increase the framework to use, 5W1H for meeting memos and BANT template for business negotiations.
