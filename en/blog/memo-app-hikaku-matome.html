2026 year memo app market overview

As of 2026, the memo app market is more diversified than ever. There are hundreds of memo apps in the "Work Efficiency" category of App Store alone, each of which is trying to establish its own position.

The following 3 is a major trend.

1. AI integration in full swing — Many major apps, such as Notion AI, Evernote AI, and Apple Intelligence, are now equipped with AI functions as standard. Automatic suggestions for summaries, tagging, and related notes are becoming common functions. However, the fullness of AI functions varies greatly from app to app.

2. Increased privacy awareness — More and more apps offer end-to-end encryption (E2EE) by default. Not only privacy-first apps like Standard Notes, but also Apple Notes has enhanced locking functions. Security comparison of memo apps is now an important selection criterion.

3. A split between focused, fast apps and feature-rich, heavier apps — All-in-one apps such as Notion and Evernote keep expanding, while lightweight apps such as SimpleMemo and Drafts also attract users by focusing on 1 thing. The launch-speed benchmark shows this difference clearly.

In order to choose the best app from such a variety of options, it is essential to objectively compare with the evaluation criteria that suit your purpose. In this article, based on the official information of each company and the measurement of the developer's startup time (iPhone 16e, warm activation), we will make a cross-comparison with 7 criteria. Since the record of the usage period is not published, it is not a real experience review.

7 evaluation criteria

The points to look at when choosing a memo app vary from person to person, but we have set the universally important 7 criteria. Each standard is evaluated at the 4 level of A (excellent), B (good), C (standard), and D (with challenges).

Standard 1
Startup speed
Time from tapping the app icon to being able to enter. Based on the warm start record (not the cold start value) measured by the developer with the iPhone 16e, 0.5 seconds or less is rated A. For more informationStartup Speed Benchmark Article.
Standard 2
UI / UX (operability)
Evaluate the intuitive ease of use at first sight, the number of steps to create, search, and organize notes, and the consistency of the design. " 5 Can you start writing notes within seconds" is important.
Standard 3
Security
End-to-end encryption (E2EE) presence or absence, local encryption, biometric authentication lock, 2 element authentication support.Detailed article on security comparison for more information.
Standard 4
Price
Scope of free use, price of paid plan, subscription vs buy-out model. " Evaluation from both sides of whether the basic functions can be used for free and the cost performance of the paid plan.
Standard 5
Offline support
Is it possible to create, edit, and view notes in an environment without an Internet connection? The reliability of automatic synchronization of notes created offline is also added. It is assumed to be used on airplanes and subways.
Standard 6
Export
Export format of memo data (Markdown, PDF, text, HTML, etc.) and the possibility of bulk export. " "Whether it will be locked in" is a very important criterion for long-term use.
Standard 7
Cross-platform
iPhone / iPad / Mac / Windows / Android / Web platform support status. Evaluate whether all devices can be synchronized seamlessly.

Detailed evaluation of each app

1. SimpleMemo（SimpleMemo）

Start 0.4 seconds, one tap transmission. " It is a memo application specialized in instantly capturing what comes to mind. Developed as an alternative to Captio, it adopts a unique workflow of sending notes to yourself by email.

Strengths: Light startup speed (0.4 seconds, developer measurement until input can be entered with warm startup), UI that has been cut to the limit, full offline support. The fact that you can take notes without interrupting your thoughts sets you apart from other apps.

Weakness: iOS only (not compatible with Android), organizing and searching for notes depends on the email client side. Image attachment and rich text editing are not supported.

People who are suitable: Those who want to eliminate the "spill" of ideas, those who want to use it as an in-box capture of GTD. For more information, please see How to use the guide.

2. Apple Notes (memo app)

A genuine memo app that is included in the iPhone from the beginning. In the 2026 version, the summary function and smart folder of Apple Intelligence have been enhanced, making it more functional than before.

Strengths: No additional installation required, perfect synchronization between Apple devices with iCloud, handwriting memo support, PDF scanning function. The UI is sophisticated and intuitive.

Weakness: It cannot be used except for Apple products (not available for Windows and Android users). Export options are limited, making it difficult to migrate a large number of notes to other apps.

Suitable people: Those who are unified with Apple products, those who do not want to install additional apps. Click here for a detailed comparison of SimpleMemo vs Apple Notes.

3. Google Keep

A free memo and task management app provided by Google. It features a card-type UI like a sticky note, and you can visually organize notes with color coding and label functions.

Strengths: Completely free, no capacity limit, Android / iOS / Web compatible cross-platform, Google with calendars and documents. The reminder function is convenient.

Limitations: Unsuitable for long notes (up to 20000 characters per 1 note), labels instead of a folder hierarchy, and limited export formats. E2EE is not provided.

Suitable people: Manage shopping lists and To-Do, use a lot of short notes, and users of the Google ecosystem. Comparison of SimpleMemo vs Google Keep is also a reference.

4. Notion

"All-in-one workspace" that integrates notes, task management, database, Wiki, and project management into 1 apps. 2026 has further strengthened the ecosystem with the evolution of Notion AI and the integration of Notion Calendar.

Strengths: A variety of views such as databases, kanbans, calendars, and galleries. It also supports template function, API cooperation, and team use. Scalability is the best among all apps.

Weakness: Slow startup, overspec to take simple notes. The cost of learning is high. Block restrictions for free plans (eased as of 2026, but not completely unlimited).

Suitable people: Those who want to centralize project management and notes, knowledge sharing in the team. Detailed comparison of SimpleMemo vs Notion.

5. Evernote

A long-established memo app. After the acquisition by Bending Spoons, it was revived with the renewal of the UI and the introduction of the AI search function. The performance of Web Clipper is still the best in the industry.

Strengths: Powerful Web Clipper, text search in PDF, OCR search for handwritten characters, and organization by tags and notebooks. Compatible with all platforms.

Weakness: Free plan restrictions are strict (up to 50 notebooks, notebook 1, 1 units). Paid plans are expensive (the current paid plans are Starter and Advanced. Check the price on the official website). The app is heavy and takes a long time to start.

Suitable people: People who have a lot of clipping of web articles, people who want to search for a large number of notes. Comparison of SimpleMemo vs Evernote.

6. Obsidian

Markdown-based knowledge base app. " With the concept of "second brain", the relationship between notes is visualized with interactive links and graph views. Since it is based on local files, the ownership of the data is entirely on the user's side.

Strengths: Local Markdown file (no vendor lock-in), 1,600 or higher plug-in, two-way link + graph view, personal use is completely free.

Weakness: The cost of learning is high for beginners, official synchronization (Obsidian Sync) is paid (Standard is $48 per year, Plus is $96 per year), and the operability of mobile apps is inferior to desktops.

Suitable people: Markdown school, researchers and writers who want to do knowledge management in earnest. Comparison of SimpleMemo vs Obsidian.

7. Bear

Beautiful Markdown editor dedicated to the Apple platform. In Bear 2.0, the editor has been greatly improved to support table, drawing, and code block highlighting.

Strengths: Beautiful UI, fast startup and search, flexible organization with hashtags, and a variety of export formats such as Markdown / PDF / HTML / DOCX.

Weakness: Apple exclusive (Windows / Android not compatible), subscription system (annual $29.99), no collaboration function.

Suitable people: Apple users who are particular about design, people who write blog manuscripts with Markdown. Comparison of SimpleMemo vs Bear.

8. Drafts

A text input hub app with the concept of "text first to Drafts". When you start it, an empty note will open immediately, and you can send the text you wrote to another app via action.

Strengths: Instant input start (startup speed comparable to SimpleMemo), powerful action function can be sent to OmniFocus, Slack, mail, Notion, etc., customization by JavaScript.

Weakness: Apple dedicated, Drafts Pro (year 2,200 yen) is required to create and edit actions, and you need to get used to your own workflow concept.

Suitable people: People who are looking for an app that will be the "starting point" of text, power users who value automation and collaboration.

9. Standard Notes

An open source memo app that puts privacy first. End-to-end encryption (E2EE) is enabled by default, and even the operating company cannot read the contents of the memo.

Strengths: E2EE standard equipment, design philosophy aiming for the durability of 100, all platform support, open source, encryption can be used even in free plans.

Weakness: You can't use editor extensions such as rich text and spreadsheets unless you have a paid plan ($90 per year). The UI is functional, but not as beautiful as the Bear.

Suit people: People who prioritize security and privacy, journalists, and professionals dealing with confidential information. Security comparison article explains in detail.

10. Microsoft OneNote

A free digital notebook app provided by Microsoft. It features a canvas-type UI that allows you to place notes in a free layout with a hierarchical structure called Notebook > Section > Page.

Strengths: Completely free, Windows / Mac / iOS / Android / Web full support, integration with Microsoft 365, free layout, handwriting support, team use with shared notebooks.

Weakness: 2.6 seconds until you can enter (measurement on this site, iPhone 16e, warm start), synchronization may be slow, export options are limited (mainly OneNote format), some people feel that the free layout is difficult to use.

Suitable people: Corporate users who use Microsoft 365, those who want to share the minutes of the meeting with the team. Comparison of SimpleMemo vs OneNote.

Recommended memo apps by purpose

"Which one is better in the end?" It depends on what you value the most. We will suggest the best app for each typical 5 use case.

Focus on speed - Start writing on the spot
If you prioritize the startup speed and the number of steps to create a memo,SimpleMemois a good fit. It launches in 0.4 seconds; write your note and press Send. Drafts also launches quickly, but SimpleMemo is ready immediately without configuring actions. It suits a GTD inbox, capturing ideas on the move, and taking meeting notes.
Recommendation: SimpleMemo > Drafts > Bear
Focus on security - Securely store confidential notes
If you are dealing with confidential information and personal journals,Standard Notesis the best choice. Since E2EE is enabled by default, it is impossible for a third party to read the data on the server. Obsidian is also based on local files, so you don't have to deposit data to the server, but iCloud or Obsidian Sync (with encryption) is required for synchronization.
Recommended: Standard Notes > Obsidian > Apple Notes
Multifunction - I want to do more than a memo
If you want to complete not only notes but also task management, database, Wiki, and project management with 1 appsNotionis one choice. The cost of learning is high, but if you can master it, you can replace almost other tools. The Evernote is also a rich feature set, but it is not as expandable as the Notion.
Recommendation: Notion > Evernote > OneNote
I want to use it for free - start with zero cost
If you want to use enough functions without spending money,Google Keepis the free app with the least restrictions. Unlimited capacity, no device limit, cross-platform support. Apple Notes is also free, but it is limited to Apple products. Obsidian is free for personal use, but there may be a charge for synchronization.
Recommendation: Google Keep > Apple Notes > Standard Notes (free version)
Business use - Sharing and collaboration with the team
If you value sharing minutes, building a team knowledge base, and cooperating with Microsoft 365OneNoteis the best. Real-time collaborative editing is possible with the shared notebook function, and integration with Microsoft Teams is also smooth. For large-scale teams, the Notion Teams plan is also a strong choice.
Recommendation: OneNote > Notion > Evernote

Summary - "The best memo app" changes depending on the purpose

Comparing the 10 app with the 7 standard, the conclusion is that "there is no best for everyone". However, if your top priority is clear, the choice will be surprisingly simple.

I will organize the points again.

  • Startup speed is the most important → SimpleMemo(0.4 seconds start)
  • Security is the most important → Standard Notes (E2EE standard)
  • Multifunctionality is the most important → Notion(all-in-one)
  • Free is the most important → Google Keep(free without restrictions)
  • Business use → OneNote (Microsoft 365 integration)
  • knowledge base construction → Obsidian(two-way link)
  • Beautiful Markdown Editor → Bear (Apple only)

Combining multiple apps is also an effective approach. For example, the workflow of "capturing ideas with SimpleMemo and organizing and managing with Notion" is supported by many users as a way to combine speed and multifunction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a memo app and a memo tool?
There is no clear difference in definition, but in this article, we do not distinguish between the "memo app" that can be written quickly and the "memo tool", which includes organization and knowledge management, and we make a cross-comparison based on the 7 standard of startup speed, UI, security, fee, offline, export, and compatible terminals.
Which memo app is the best after all?
It changes depending on the purpose. If you value speed, SimpleMemo, if you value multi-function, Notion, if it is free, Google Keep, if you value security, Standard Notes, and OneNote for business use. For details, please refer to the comparison table above and "Recommended by Purpose".
Which memo app can I use for free?
Apple Notes, Google Keep, Joplin, etc. can be used for free.Free memo app rankingfor more options. SimpleMemo is also available at no charge, with up to 3 messages per day on the Free plan.
Do you have an open source memo app?
Joplin, Logseq, and Standard Notes are open source. SimpleMemo is not open source, but it emphasizes verifiable transparency through on-device AES-GCM encryption and a design that does not persistently store note bodies on the server. For differences in encryption, see Encryption comparison.
