5 Free Memo Apps
Choose by Workflow and Limits

Compare five options by device, content, and where you want to review a note. A notebook for photos and a tool for sending thoughts to your inbox serve different needs.

By AI ATAKA · Updated September 7, 2026

Disclosure: The author develops Simple Memo. These are use-case suggestions based on official feature and plan information, without comparative speed or security scores. Simple Memo is for iPhone, focuses on text, and has a free limit of 3 sends per day.

Three decisions before choosing

  • Device: Start with Apple Notes for an Apple workflow; consider Google Keep, Simplenote, or Notion for Android and browser access.
  • Content: Decide whether you need photos and handwriting or mainly short text.
  • Destination: Keep a notebook or send thoughts to an inbox you already review. An existing Notion workspace or Obsidian vault can remain your place for organizing knowledge.

Apple Notes: text and images together

A practical starting point for checklists, document scans, and handwritten notes on Apple devices. Some features require iCloud notes or on-device notes and are unavailable with other email accounts.

See Apple’s Notes guide and iCloud storage. Notes share cloud capacity with other data; this is not unlimited dedicated note storage.

Google Keep: short notes across devices

Useful for short notes, lists, and photos that you want to revisit on a phone and in a browser. Labels, colors, and pins help you find them.

For a longer document, Keep provides a route to Google Docs. See Google’s feature overview. For a work account, also check your organization’s usage rules.

Simplenote: keep the text simple

A free service from Automattic with Markdown, tags, and device sync. Once signed in and notes are downloaded, the apps support offline editing.

Consider another option for file attachments or particularly sensitive information: Simplenote’s help says notes are not encrypted at rest. See its official feature, offline, security, and export guidance.

Notion Free: develop notes into a workspace

Useful when notes become structured pages, tables, and databases. If you already review information in Notion, keeping it as your destination can avoid adding another review routine.

The official Free plan has unlimited pages and blocks for individuals, a 5 MB per-file upload limit, and 7-day history. Workspaces with two or more members have a separate block limit.

Simple Memo: capture into your inbox

Send text from an iPhone to a configured email address. For image notebooks, handwriting, or team editing, consider the options above. Obsidian users can also review the vault integration setup.

The free tier permits 3 sends/day. The Outbox and send history are encrypted on the device; the message passes through delivery infrastructure when sent. The inbox is not the only place data is held, and the service is not E2EE. See the privacy policy.

Check before committing to an app

Estimate your daily volume, attachments, and devices. Try saving, finding, and exporting a small note before moving a collection. Offline storage, device sync, and completed email delivery are different states.

Consider a paid plan when your actual workflow exceeds the free scope. Choose a place you will revisit instead of relying on a universal ranking.

Frequently asked questions

Are any memo apps completely free?
Simplenote is a free service. Apple Notes and Google Keep also cover basic personal note-taking. Related services, such as additional iCloud storage, may cost extra.
Which free app should I try on iPhone?
Consider Apple Notes for photos, scans, and handwriting; Google Keep or Simplenote for Android and web access; and Notion for structured pages and tables.
How many Simple Memo sends are free?
The free tier allows 3 sends per day. It suits sending text from an iPhone to your inbox. Sending more requires a paid plan.
Does offline capture mean the email has arrived?
No. Local storage, device sync, and email delivery are separate. Simple Memo keeps pending memos in its Outbox. Check the destination inbox to confirm delivery.
