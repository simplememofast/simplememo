Captioo vs Simple Memo - for Obsidian
For people who want to email themselves, the Captio way

If you searched for "Captio" on the App Store and saw both Captioo and "Simple Memo - for Obsidian" side by side, this article is for you. Captio, Captioo, and Simple Memo are three separate apps. Here is what each is, and how to choose.

By AI ATAKA · Published: May 1, 2026

TL;DR

Captio, Captioo, and Simple Memo - for Obsidian are three separate apps. Simple Memo is not the official successor to Captio and is unrelated to Captioo (808 inc.). Captioo leans toward more features (image, widgets) and is free; Simple Memo focuses narrowly on "type and email it to yourself."

Disclosure: The author (AI ATAKA) is the developer of Simple Memo. This article compares Captioo and Simple Memo, so please note possible bias. Information about Captioo is based on its public App Store description; we avoid definitive judgments about it.

First: Captio, Captioo, and Simple Memo - for Obsidian are all different apps

The original Captio was a focused iPhone app for one thing: launch, type, and send the note to your own email. Not a note organizer — a way to flush ideas into your inbox before you forgot them. People who loved that lightness still miss it.

Today, when you search "Captio" on the App Store or on Google, you may see Captioo and Simple Memo - for Obsidian appear together. The names are similar, but these are three separate apps:

  • Captio: A former iPhone app that sent memos to your own email. No longer available on the App Store.
  • Captioo: An app from a different developer (808 inc.), available on App Store and Google Play.
  • Simple Memo - for Obsidian: This site's app. An independent iPhone app inspired by Captio's workflow.

Simple Memo is unrelated to Captio and its developer, Captioo, 808 inc., and Emburse Captio. Simple Memo is not an official successor to Captio. It is an independent app inspired by the "write and email it to yourself" workflow that made Captio loved.

What is Captioo

Captioo is an app on the App Store and Google Play for sending memos and ideas to your own email. According to its public store description, it offers:

  • One-tap memo to email
  • Image sending
  • Widget support
  • Sharing from Safari and Chrome
  • Free to use

If a free price tag, image sending, or sharing features matter to you, Captioo may be a good fit. When choosing an app, factors like "how do ads sit with me," "how simple is it to actually use," and "how close to Captio's minimal flow does it feel" also matter — and these depend on personal preference.

* This article's description of Captioo is based on its public App Store listing. For current features, pricing, and ad behavior, please refer to the latest store page.

What is Simple Memo - for Obsidian

Simple Memo is an iPhone app focused on one thing: writing a memo and sending it straight to your own email inbox. The flow is small:

  1. Open the app
  2. Write the memo
  3. Send it to your email

It is not for foldering, tagging, or managing notes as a database. It only does one thing — get the thought into your inbox before it disappears. Simple Memo is not a replacement for Apple Notes, Notion, Drafts, Todoist, or Things. It sits one step before them: the first place a fleeting idea lands, before you ever decide where it really belongs.

If you miss Captio's feel

Most people who loved Captio didn't want a "powerful note app." They wanted a much simpler experience:

  • Fast to open
  • Fast to start typing
  • Fast to send
  • It shows up in your inbox afterwards
  • You don't have to manage notes inside the app

Simple Memo leans toward that. It is not a replacement for Captioo, Drafts, Apple Notes, Notion, Todoist, or Things. It sits one step earlier — the first place a thought lands.

Free apps vs paid apps

Captioo is listed on the store as a free app. If a free option is your priority, trying Captioo first is reasonable.

The Free plan gives you 3 sends/day, free forever. Premium removes that limit and unlocks unlimited sending.

Japan pricing:

  • Monthly: ¥500
  • Yearly: ¥5,000

English-region pricing:

  • Premium Monthly: $2.99 / month
  • Premium Yearly: $29.99 / year

Prices may vary by region and App Store display. Billing and cancellation are handled via Apple's App Store / In-App Purchase.

Which one should you pick

Captioo may fit you if:

  • You want to use a free app
  • You want image sending and sharing features
  • You value extras like widgets and a broader feature set

Simple Memo may fit you if:

  • You want to type and email it to yourself, Captio-style
  • Sending a text memo as fast as possible is enough
  • You don't want to manage notes inside the app
  • You want your inbox to be the holding pen for fleeting thoughts
  • You prefer a light feel without extra navigation

Captioo is the right pick for some people. But if what you want is just the light, Captio-like "write it and email it to yourself" experience, Simple Memo is also worth a look.

FAQ

Is Simple Memo the official successor to Captio?

No. Simple Memo is not Captio's official successor. It is an independent iPhone app, with no affiliation with Captio or its developer.

Is Simple Memo related to Captioo?

No. Simple Memo is not affiliated with Captioo or 808 inc., and is also unrelated to Emburse Captio.

Are Captioo and Captio the same app?

No. Despite the similar names, Captioo and the original Captio are different apps from different developers.

Captioo's ads bother me. How is Simple Memo different?

Ad behavior and overall feel can vary by version and environment. Simple Memo aims for a simple design centered on "type and email it to yourself," and tries to keep the write-and-send path free of extras. For current behavior, please refer to the App Store listing or the app itself.

Is Simple Memo free?

The Free plan gives you 3 sends per day, free forever. Premium unlocks unlimited sending. Japan pricing is ¥500 / month or ¥5,000 / year. English-region pricing is $2.99 / month or $29.99 / year.

Where can I cancel?

You can cancel from iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Cancellation cannot be done from within the app.
