Is LINE Keep Memo still available?
Find your notes and choose where to keep them

Keep Memo still works. The original LINE Keep ended. Here is how to find Keep Memo and choose a suitable home for the things you want to save.

By AI ATAKA, Simple Memo Developer · Published March 16, 2026 · Updated September 6, 2026

What ended? Open Keep Memo Save your content Compare five options FAQ

LINE Keep ended; Keep Memo continues

The original LINE Keep closed on August 28, 2024. Keep Memo is a separate self-chat. LINE’s official announcement explains the switch in sharing destinations.

How to find Keep Memo

On your primary phone, open LINE → Home → your profile → Keep Memo. Update LINE if missing. To pin it in Chats, swipe right on iPhone or long-press on Android.

If hidden, restore it under Home → Settings → Chats → Hidden chats. See LINE Help for device-specific instructions.

Save accessible photos, videos and files elsewhere

LINE limits server retention for Keep Memo images, videos and files without publishing an exact duration. Save important items while accessible. LINE’s download instructions.

The original Keep export is no longer available. Work with accessible Keep Memo content or copies you previously downloaded.

Start by copying one item and checking it opens at the destination. Then move the rest. Keep another copy of important data, and account for storage space, account access and backups even when using email or cloud services.

Choose by what you want to save

Choose the formats you need and where you will revisit them. You can also keep using Keep Memo and copy selected items to another destination.

Try one note before moving a collection

A new app will not help if you do not know where to look later. Save one item, such as a link to revisit at the weekend, and try to find it the next day. Check that photos open and text is easy to reuse.

If you are considering Obsidian, the free Inbox Markdown generator lets you preview bullets or checkboxes. Copy or download the result and insert it yourself. It does not import from LINE or automatically write to a vault.

Frequently asked questions

Did Keep Memo shut down too?
No. Keep Memo is separate from the discontinued LINE Keep service and remains available.
Are Keep Memo photos kept forever?
Media retention is limited. Save accessible copies elsewhere.
Are LINE Keep and Google Keep the same?
They are separate services. The LINE Keep shutdown does not require moving your Google Keep notes.
What if I want pictures and text in the same app?
Choose an app that supports pictures, such as Apple Notes or Google Keep. For video and work files, check supported formats and capacity. Simple Memo focuses on text.
