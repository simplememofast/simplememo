Choose by Use, Limits, and Sharing

Start with what you capture and where you will use it again. Compare 20 apps by input, organization, collaboration, storage, and export conditions.

By AI Ataka · Last updated: September 9, 2026

Useful starting points are Apple Notes / OneNote for handwriting and images, Google Keep / Simplenote for short notes, Notion for team documents, and Obsidian for Markdown files. Consider Simple Memo when you want short text delivered to an inbox or configured note destination.

The author develops Simple Memo. This use-case comparison draws on official documentation reviewed September 9, 2026. It does not claim device testing of all 20 apps or an overall ranking. Speed records are documented separately with their measurement conditions.

Which note apps are worth paying for in 2026?

A paid plan is worth considering when it provides a feature you need and a trial or small test demonstrates its value.Consider attachments, sync, sharing permissions, recovery history, and AI capacity as well as note counts.

For example, Drafts distinguishes running actions from creating or editing them, while Bear makes features such as sync part of Pro. Standard Notes offers E2EE text sync free. Neither encryption nor use on a single device alone determines whether you need a paid plan.

Use the official links below to check current prices, billing periods, and region. Distinguish a monthly equivalent from an annual upfront charge, and check education or existing-contract terms separately.

Shortlist two candidates for your workflow

Handwriting, images, and class notes

Try the pen input, scans, and PDFs you actually use in Apple Notes and OneNote. Features vary by device and storage account, so open the same material on your target iPad or PC. For text alone, Simplenote or Google Keep may be enough.

Teams and small-business sharing

In Notion or OneNote, test viewer, editor, and administrator access and recovery of the history you need. Obsidian Sync shares files, but it does not show collaborators’ cursors or provide live editing of the same file. Every collaborator needs Sync, and fine-grained permissions are not supported.

Research, writing, and connected knowledge

Try Obsidian for files you manage directly, Roam Research or Logseq for linked outlines, Heptabase for spatial cards, and Anytype or Tana for typed objects and fields. Export a few connected notes to check what happens to links, tags, and layout outside the app.

Short text into an inbox or Obsidian

Simple Memo sends short text to a configured recipient or note destination. Verify the address, configure the vault or target note, and confirm the first result at the destination. Automatic saving to Notion also requires Notion integration setup. Copying into Standard Notes manually is a separate step.

Offline access and privacy

Writing offline, syncing to another device, and provider-inaccessible content are separate requirements. Download needed Notion pages in the app beforehand. Check Standard Notes E2EE, Joplin encryption settings, or Anytype sync mode against your actual storage needs.

AI search, summaries, and task organization

Choose the specific feature in tools such as Notion, Evernote, or Mem, then inspect included notes, connected sources, and retention. A no-training policy does not mean content is never processed to answer a request. Plans may also meter voice time or requests rather than just note count.

Five checks before paying or switching

  1. Create a few short notes, a longer note, and an attachment.
  2. Find the note again using words you would naturally search for.
  3. Prepare needed pages, go offline, edit, and check changes after reconnecting.
  4. Open it on your other device or with the collaborator permissions you intend to use.
  5. Export a few notes and verify content, attachments, dates, and links at the destination.

Check these five things and the cost before moving your full collection. If your current app meets your needs, you do not need another one. If you separate capture from organization, decide which transfer steps are configured and which are manual.

Evaluate speed on your actual capture path

Launch time depends on hardware, OS, app state, content, and which steps count as ready to type. Do not mix figures from different conditions into a universal fastest-app claim.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best note app?

Consider Apple Notes or OneNote for handwriting and images, Notion for team documents, and Obsidian for Markdown files. Simple Memo is an option for short text sent to an inbox or configured note destination. Pick one use case and test two candidates.

Which note apps can I use free?

Candidates include basic use of Apple Notes, Google Keep, Simplenote, and Obsidian, plus Standard Notes text sync. Check paid sync services, cloud capacity, attachments, and collaboration separately.

Is Evernote Free limited to 50 notes per month?

No. The documented free limit is 50 total notes and one notebook, not 50 additional notes every month. Check device and other limits too.

Does Obsidian Sync support live collaborative editing?

Shared vaults share files, but live collaborative editing of the same file is not supported. Every participant needs Sync, and fine-grained permissions are not supported.

Does encryption in an app make forwarded email E2EE?

Not necessarily. Local encryption, E2EE sync, and ordinary email delivery have different scopes. Simple Memo’s ordinary email route involves content processing by its sending API and delivery provider.

When should I switch to a paid plan?

Consider paying when a needed feature or capacity is outside the free tier and a small test demonstrates its value. Sharing controls, recovery history, attachments, or sync can matter even if you do not hit a daily limit.
