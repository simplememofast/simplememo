Which Memo App Is Right for You?
A 2026 Decision Flowchart

"There are too many memo apps -- I can't choose." This guide solves that in 30 seconds. Answer a few questions about how you take notes, and the flowchart points you to the right app. Plus, we include a quick-reference table comparing 12 apps across speed, organization, privacy, and price.

By AI ATAKA · Published: March 8, 2026

TL;DR

Can't decide on a memo app? This flowchart gives you the answer in 30 seconds. "Quick capture" leads to Simple Memo - for Obsidian/Drafts, "organize knowledge" to Obsidian/Notion, "team sharing" to Notion/OneNote, "free" to Apple Notes/Google Keep, "privacy" to Simple Memo - for Obsidian/Standard Notes. A decision guide that matches your workflow to the right tool.

Memo App Decision Flowchart

Q1: What is the main purpose of your notes?
A. Capture ideas before I forget → Go to Q2
B. Organize and structure my knowledge → Go to Q3
C. Share with my team → Go to Q4
Q2: Where do you want your notes saved?

Q3: What kind of structure do you need?
Linked notes (Zettelkasten) → Obsidian / Logseq

2-5 people → Notion / Google Keep
10+ people → Notion / OneNote
Entire company → OneNote / Confluence

C. Privacy-First (Encryption & Security)

For business secrets, personal journals, medical notes, or anything you don't want others to see, encryption is non-negotiable. Choose between "no server copy of memo bodies" and "end-to-end encryption."

Simple Memo - for Obsidian (Captio-style)No server copy of memo bodies
Sends memos via Cloudflare Workers and Resend without keeping memo bodies on our servers. The offline Outbox is protected with AES-GCM encryption. No account registration is needed (the address you send to is verified with a one-time code). This reduces what a server breach could expose.
AES-GCM encryption - no server copy of memo bodiesiOS onlyFor reducing data breach exposure by design
Standard NotesE2E Encrypted
End-to-end encryption included on the free tier. Open-source, third-party audited. Works on all platforms (iOS, Android, Web, Windows, Mac, Linux). Paid plans add rich text editors, code editor, and spreadsheets.
E2E encryption - open sourceLimited free featuresFor privacy-first users
JoplinOpen Source
Fully open-source note app with E2E encryption support. Sync with your own server (Nextcloud, Dropbox, OneDrive, etc.). Markdown support and Web Clipper included. Ideal for users who want complete control over where their data is stored.

E. When Simple Memo - for Obsidian Is NOT the Right Choice (Honest Assessment)

Simple Memo - for Obsidian is purpose-built for "think it, type it, email it." That focus means it's not the right tool for everyone. Here's an honest look at when you should use something else.

Long-Form Writing
Simple Memo - for Obsidian is built for short, fast captures. Reports over 1,000 characters, meeting minutes, and blog drafts need a proper writing environment. Try Obsidian's focus mode or Bear's Markdown editor instead.
Team Collaboration
Simple Memo - for Obsidian is designed for personal use via email -- there are no team sharing features. For team knowledge sharing, Notion's team spaces are ideal. For large enterprises, consider OneNote or Confluence.
Image & File Attachments
Simple Memo - for Obsidian is text-only. Photos, screenshots, PDFs, and other file attachments are not supported. For rich media notes, use Evernote or OneNote.
Android Users
Simple Memo - for Obsidian is currently iOS-only. Android users should try Google Keep (free, simple) or Simplenote (free, Markdown). For cross-platform needs, Notion is also worth considering.

FAQ

Should I pick just one memo app?

No -- using 2-3 apps by purpose is the 2026 best practice. "Capture" and "organize" require different features. For example, Simple Memo - for Obsidian for quick capture + Obsidian for organization is a powerful combination. See the "Recommended Combinations" section in our Best Memo Apps 2026 article for more details.

What criteria were used to create this flowchart?

The primary axis is "purpose of use." We evaluated 12 apps across five dimensions: speed, organization, privacy, price, and platform support. Capture speeds were measured on actual devices. See our individual app comparison pages for detailed breakdowns.

Why does this guide include cases where Simple Memo - for Obsidian is NOT recommended?

Because the goal of this page is to help you find the right app -- not to sell Simple Memo - for Obsidian to everyone. Simple Memo - for Obsidian excels at instant capture and email delivery but is not designed for long-form writing, team sharing, or file attachments. Being honest builds trust and ensures Simple Memo - for Obsidian reaches users who truly benefit from it. Check our use cases page to see where Simple Memo - for Obsidian shines.
