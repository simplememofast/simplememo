What Was Captio?

The Pioneer of One-Tap Email2010-2024

Captio was an iOS app launched around 2010, laser-focused on a single concept: instantly sending whatever you typed to your own email address. Open the app, type your thought, tap send -- done. No folders, no categories, no tags. Its uncompromising simplicity was its greatest feature.

Captio was especially beloved by GTD (Getting Things Done) practitioners and busy professionals. The workflow of "capture an idea and send it to your inbox in 2 seconds" was featured in countless productivity blogs and podcasts, sometimes called "the perfect memo app."

The UI was stripped to the absolute minimum -- launch the app and the cursor immediately focused on the text field. Enter a subject, write the body, and hit send. No extra features, no distractions. True to its name ("Captio" means "capture"), it was solely dedicated to catching thoughts and delivering them to email.

Why Captio Disappeared

App Store exit and shutdown: According to the developer's notice, Captio left the App Store about two years before the notice, and the cloud service for existing users ended on October 1, 2024. New downloads are impossible, and the service that delivered its emails has stopped.

Developer Stopped Updating: Captio's last update dated back several years. iOS undergoes major annual updates with new APIs and security requirements, but Captio failed to keep pace. Apps left unmaintained for extended periods eventually fail to meet Apple's App Store review guidelines and become subject to removal.

iOS Compatibility Issues: Starting with iOS 16, apps relying on deprecated APIs increasingly experienced malfunctions. Captio is believed to have faced compatibility issues with its email sending functionality and background processing on newer iOS versions. Users reported problems like "the send button does nothing" and "the app crashes immediately after launch."

The Developer's Shutdown Notice: The developer (Tupil / Ben Lenarts) published an end-of-life notice on captio.co, announcing the cloud service would end on October 1, 2024. Even so, awareness of the App Store removal was limited, and many users only noticed when the app stopped working.

What Captio Users Lost

Instant Launch-to-Email Workflow: Captio's essence was the ultra-fast workflow of "open the app the moment an idea strikes, send it to email in seconds." No other memo or email app could replicate this speed. Ideas are freshest the moment they appear, and Captio was the perfect tool for capturing them before they fade.

Ultimate Simplicity: No folders, no categories, no tags, no search. Captio had exactly two functions: write a memo and send it. This radical simplicity reduced decision-making cost to zero. No "which folder should I use?" or "what tag should I apply?" -- just write and send. This minimal cognitive load was Captio's greatest value.

Reliable Offline Queue: Captio could queue memos even without connectivity and auto-send them when back online. You could confidently take notes during subway commutes or on flights without worrying about forgetting to send them later.

What Captio Users Will Notice

As a former Captio user, here's an honest assessment of what's the same, what's better, and what's different about Simple Memo.

What Feels Exactly the Same

The "Open, Write, Send" experience is identical: The moment you first use Simple Memo, you'll think "this is Captio." The cursor focuses on the text field immediately, you type, you hit send. No menus, no sidebars, no folders. Captio's signature "don't interrupt the thought" design philosophy is fully preserved.

Zero-decision simplicity: No "which folder?" or "what tag?" decisions needed. Like Captio, Simple Memo delivers "zero cognitive load memo capture." It remains the ideal "empty your mind instantly" tool for GTD practitioners.

Email-based data management: Just like Captio, all memos are sent as emails. No proprietary cloud service dependency. Your memos permanently reside in your email account. "Data survives even if the app dies" -- Captio's best design choice, carried forward.

What's Better

Encryption adds peace of mind: Simple Memo implements AES-GCM encryption, which Captio never had. Offline-queued memos are encrypted, so even if your device is lost, pending memos can't be read by third parties. This is especially valuable for business users handling sensitive notes.

Evolved offline queue: Captio's offline feature was basic -- write offline, send when connected. Simple Memo's Outbox architecture adds auto-retry for unstable connections and visual queue status. No more wondering "did it send or not?"

Modern UI design: Captio's interface was rooted in the iOS 6 era (2012). Simple Memo follows the latest iOS design guidelines with dark mode support. Same minimalist philosophy, but a much more polished visual experience.

Transparent zero-retention: Whether Captio retained data on servers was unclear. Simple Memo explicitly uses a zero-retention architecture via Cloudflare Workers. For privacy-conscious users, this transparency matters.

What's Honestly Different

Pricing model: Captio was a one-time purchase of a few dollars. Simple Memo uses a subscription model -- free tier (3 sends/day) or premium ($2.99/month, unlimited). The ongoing cost is a fair trade-off for sustained development and server infrastructure (email sending requires real infrastructure costs).

Brand familiarity: After years of using "Captio," switching to "Simple Memo - for Obsidian" may feel unfamiliar. That's understandable. But the "launch, write, send" experience itself hasn't changed.

No attachments: Like Captio, Simple Memo doesn't support file attachments. If you need to send images with memos, you'll need to use a regular email app. Simple Memo intentionally focuses on text-only capture by design.

Migration FAQ

Can I migrate my Captio data?

All memos sent through Captio are already in your email inbox as regular emails. No data "migration" is needed. Just install Simple Memo, set the same email address, and your Captio memos and Simple Memo memos will coexist in the same inbox. One of Captio's best design choices was "never trap memos inside the app." Thanks to that, your data is completely safe even after Captio's shutdown.

Can I add a subject line?

Yes, just like Captio, Simple Memo supports optional subject lines. The subject field is optional -- you can send without one. GTD workflows like "project name in the subject" work exactly as they did with Captio.

Can I send to multiple recipients?

Simple Memo currently supports a single recipient email address, same as Captio. The design rationale is that "sending memos to yourself" doesn't require multiple recipients. You can change the recipient address anytime in settings.

Does it work with Gmail, iCloud Mail, etc.?

Yes, Simple Memo works with any email service -- Gmail, iCloud Mail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, corporate email servers, and more. Like Captio, it doesn't depend on the recipient's email service.

How much can I use for free?

The free plan allows 3 memo sends per day, free forever. For "jot down a few ideas throughout the day" usage, this is sufficient. The premium plan ($2.99/month) unlocks unlimited sends. Unlike Captio's one-time purchase, Simple Memo uses a subscription model, but the basic workflow is free.

Is there an Android version?

Currently, Simple Memo is iOS only -- same as Captio was. Android development is being considered but has no confirmed timeline. Android users may want to explore alternatives like Email Me App or Mail to Self.

Can Simple Memo fully recreate the Captio experience?

Captio's core experience -- "open the app, instantly start writing, one-tap send" -- is fully replicated in Simple Memo. Launch speed is 0.4 seconds, comparable to Captio; sending is one-tap. The visual design is different (modernized), but the operational flow is identical. Many Captio users report a seamless transition.

Why is Captio no longer available?

The developer announced on captio.co that Captio's cloud service would end on October 1, 2024. According to that notice, the app had already left the App Store about two years earlier, and the developer could no longer dedicate the resources needed to keep it stable and secure.

What happened to my memos sent through Captio?

All memos sent through Captio remain safely in your email inbox. Captio sent memos directly as email rather than storing them within the app, so the app's discontinuation does not mean data loss. This was one of Captio's best design decisions.
