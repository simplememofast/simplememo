Opens the App Store

The Japanese App Store lists this app as “シンプルメモ - Obsidian連携・高速音声入力”, formerly “Simple Memo - for Obsidian”. It automatically adds titles, tags and note types. The Free plan allows up to three sends per day.

Three minutes after installing

  1. Speak it — or type it

    The app opens with the cursor already in the field. Tap the mic and talk — transcription happens on the device. Don't bother tidying the wording.

  2. It arrives in your own inbox

    One tap sends it to the address you set. Offline, it waits encrypted in the Outbox and goes out by itself when you're back on a network.

  3. It stacks up in Obsidian

    Set up the Obsidian integration once and every memo appends itself to a note in your vault. Setup steps here.
